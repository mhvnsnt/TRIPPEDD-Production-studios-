# KIKO — Pier Song Lyrics (draft 2026-10-11)

## Character voice
Kiko speaks mostly Japanese, broken English when he tries. Calm, measured,
theatrical. He means every word. The comedy is that he's dead serious.

## What the song is about (without saying it)
- He's never where he's supposed to be (the ghost, wrong pier, missed meeting)
- The robe is his identity (white fur, the thing people remember)
- The mist / the breath / the stillness before he acts (Muta DNA)
- The empty theater — he performs anyway, for nobody, for the moon
- He doesn't explain himself. He just... arrives. Late. Perfect.

## Lyrics

### [0:00-0:08] SCENE (the 8s — EP01 version)
*(Spoken, theatrical, unbothered. Foghorn underneath.)*

**KIKO:**
"The theater is empty. Encore anyway."
*(Japanese, soft:)* "誰もいない。もう一度。"
(Dare mo inai. Mou ichido. — Nobody's here. Once more.)

### [0:08-0:30] VERSE 1
*(Quiet. Piano + soft strings. He's looking at the water.)*

**KIKO:**
*(Japanese)*
"月が出ている。白い毛皮は濡れていない。"
(Tsuki ga dete iru. Shiroi kegawa wa nurete inai.)
(The moon is out. The white fur isn't wet.)

"会議はどこだ？ああ、違う埠頭だ。"
(Kaigi wa doko da? Aa, chigau hatou da.)
(Where's the meeting? Ah. Wrong pier.)

*(Broken English, earnest)*
"They say... Kiko, come. Kiko, now.
I come... to the water. I come... how?"

### [0:30-0:52] PRE-CHORUS
*(Building. Choir enters. He stands, robe catches the wind.)*

**KIKO:**
*(Japanese, stronger)*
"ゆっくり歩け。急ぐな。霧が先に行く。"
(Yukkuri aruke. Isogu na. Kiri ga saki ni iku.)
(Walk slowly. Don't hurry. The mist goes first.)

*(Broken English)*
"Ghost... they call me ghost.
Ghost is never late. Ghost is... early. For the moon."

### [0:52-1:14] CHORUS
*(Full swell. His big moment. He means ALL of this.)*

**KIKO:**
*(Japanese)*
"見てくれ、このローブを！白い！きれいだろう？"
(Mite kure, kono ROOBU wo! Shiroi! Kirei darou?)
(Look at this robe! White! Isn't it beautiful?)

*(Broken English, belting)*
"Check out... my robe, guys! It's nice!
It's nice, isn't it?! LOOK!"

*(Japanese, quieter, almost to himself)*
"誰も見ていない。いいんだ。"
(Dare mo mite inai. Iin da.)
(Nobody's watching. That's fine.)

### [1:14-1:36] VERSE 2
*(Darker. He's thinking about the others.)*

**KIKO:**
*(Japanese)*
"あいつらは会議中だ。怒っているだろう。"
(Aitsura wa kaigi-chuu da. Okotte iru darou.)
(They're in the meeting. They're probably angry.)

"静寂は武器だ。待つことも武器だ。"
(Seijaku wa buki da. Matsu koto mo buki da.)
(Stillness is a weapon. Waiting is also a weapon.)

*(Broken English)*
"Static... he shouts. He shouts my name.
K-I-K-O. I hear... the water. Louder."

### [1:36-1:58] PRE-CHORUS 2 → CHORUS 2
*(Compressed, driving. He's moving now, robe flowing.)*

**KIKO:**
*(Japanese)*
"息を吸って... 吐く。"
(Iki wo sutte... haku.)
(Breathe in... breathe out.)

*(Broken English, building)*
"The theater... is empty!
Encore! ENCORE! Anyway!"

*(Full chorus repeat, bigger)*
"見てくれ、このローブを！白い！きれいだろう？"
"Check out... my robe, guys! It's nice!
It's nice, isn't it?! LOOK!"

### [1:58-2:10] BRIDGE
*(Quietest moment. Almost a cappella piano. He's small against the night.)*

**KIKO:**
*(Japanese, barely a whisper)*
"お父さんが... 見ているかな。"
(Otousan ga... mite iru kana.)
(I wonder... if father's watching.)

*(Beat. Then, stronger:)*
"いや。月が見ている。それでいい。"
(Iya. Tsuki ga mite iru. Sore de ii.)
(No. The moon is watching. That's enough.)

### [2:10-2:30] FINAL CHORUS + OUTRO
*(Everything comes back. Biggest he's ever been. Then the slam.)*

**KIKO:**
*(Japanese + English, alternating, triumphant)*
"誰もいない！(Nobody's here!)
もう一度！(Once more!)
Check out my robe!
It's nice, ISN'T IT?!"

*(Final line, calm again. The stillness returns.)*
"Encore anyway."

*(Dm slam. Silence. Water.)*

---

## Notes for the vocal builder
- Kiko's Japanese is native-fluent, calm, measured — NOT anime-excited
- His English is broken but CONFIDENT — he doesn't know it's broken
- The "LOOK!" and "ISN'T IT?!" moments are belted, theatrical, 100% earnest
- The bridge ("Otousan...") is the only vulnerable moment — quiet, real
  (Muta DNA: the "I miss daddy" backstage crack — the demon has a father)
- He never winks at the audience. The comedy is that he doesn't know he's funny.
