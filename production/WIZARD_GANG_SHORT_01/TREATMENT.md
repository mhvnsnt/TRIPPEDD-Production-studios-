# Treatment — WIZARD_GANG_SHORT_01 "The Council Rises"

**Runtime:** ~50 seconds · **Ratio:** 9:16 primary

## Logline

The robed council of the Hollows steps out of the dark, one color at a time — and the last robe looks straight at you.

## Premise

A ~50-second staged entrance cinematic (El Toro de Oro style) introducing the Wizard Gang to the TRIPPEDD audience. No exposition. No dialogue until the end — and even then, no spoken lines: the Narrator's voice is pending the owner's own recording, so the Narrator speaks with his eyes only.

The short follows the El Toro de Oro grammar: **environment → figure → detail → movement → direct address → title card.** Darkness first, then controlled light, then the colors.

## Beats (9 shots, ~5.5s each)

1. **The Hollows at night (0–5s).** Rooftop. Hooded silhouettes backlit in purple on the skyline. Green and purple cauldron fires burn at gathering spots below. An "IT SEES" tag breathes on a wall. The world is introduced before any person.
2. **ASHES (5–11s).** Scarlet robe. Ram-horn dread braids under the hood. Gold chains. Slow camera orbit; he turns with it — measured, unhurried. Secret leader energy: he doesn't perform, he presides.
3. **ONYX (11–16s).** Green robe. Green cauldron-light from below, smoke drifting. The public face of the gang.
4. **ECHO (16–21s).** Pink robe, graffiti-patterned. The gold "ECHO" pendant catches the light. Street-wizard mashup at full tilt.
5. **STATIC + CIPHER (21–27s).** Deep blue robe beside the yellow robe. Two glints each in the dark, side by side. The "SWMG" pendant — the acronym lives on jewelry, never as spoken or in-game text.
6. **HOLLOW (27–32s).** Orange robe. The gold "HOLLOW" pendants. The Hollows district itself, personified.
7. **SOMBRA NEGRA (32–37s).** Black robe with purple trim on the edges. The mercenary's silhouette at the council's edge — present, unaffiliated, watching.
8. **The 9th seat (37–42s).** White fur robe. The gold "KIKO" pendant. Title-card whisper: the ninth seat is sometimes empty. Sometimes it isn't.
9. **THE NARRATOR (42–50s).** Purple robe. Void-black face, sparkly eye glints, diamond-grill smile, blonde braids, gold chains. The camera pushes to his face and he looks **straight at the viewer** — at the player, not at a character. This is his sanctioned 4th-wall beat: the council's public reveal is a story-progression moment. Title cards: "WIZARD GANG" → "TRIPPEDD".

## Tone

Dead serious. No comedy in this one — the Wizard Gang segment is the ritual counterweight inside TRIPPEDD's genre-fluid comedy. Ritual, presence, gold, fire, the color-blocked council. The menace is quiet. The grins are diamond.
