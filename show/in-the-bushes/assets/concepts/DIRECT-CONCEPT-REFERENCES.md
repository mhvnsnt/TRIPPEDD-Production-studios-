# In the Bushes — Direct Character Concept References

## Purpose

This document records character-design references supplied directly by the showrunner during production. These are **reference direction**, not final traced artwork or a replacement for the production SVG assets.

## Busch

The supplied hand-drawn Busch concept establishes a deliberately handmade, irregular bush creature rather than a geometric mascot. Key visual signals:

- dense, uneven leafy silhouette;
- large expressive eyes with asymmetry/uneven placement encouraged;
- simple, readable mouth/nose marks;
- exaggerated facial expression as a major comedy tool;
- bright green foliage as the dominant body identity;
- red shoes as a distinctive character accent;
- rough marker/crayon energy is useful as a style reference even when production assets become cleaner;
- the wordmark sketch reads `Busch` and is treated as the character's name reference, not beverage-company artwork.

Production rule: preserve the **organic bush silhouette and handmade personality** when refining the reusable Busch rig. Do not turn the character into a rigid geometric blob merely to simplify animation.

## Mr. Gold

The supplied concept establishes Mr. Gold as a separate purple character with a deliberately crude, expressive cartoon construction:

- tall purple body with a rounded/irregular silhouette;
- very simple thin arms and legs;
- oversized red hat with a gold/tan central accent;
- large expressive eyes;
- wide grin with visible gold/tan tooth shapes;
- intentionally rough proportions and line quality;
- strong color contrast that makes the character readable immediately.

Mr. Gold is **not yet wired into EP01's origin render**. Keep him as a separate character until his story role, rig, and production states are defined.

## Production handling

The original concept images were supplied directly in the production conversation. This file preserves their design intent without claiming that the rough references are the final animation assets. Future clean model sheets should be derived from the show's approved character direction rather than mechanically tracing the sketches.
