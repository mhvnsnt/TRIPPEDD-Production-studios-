# Steal Like An Artist — Luck of the Irish
## Study materials for transformation sequences

> LOTI is built on transformation: the leprechaun build-up, freeze-frame,
> and Looney Tunes payoff. These materials cover how pros structure
> transformation scenes and horror-to-comedy tone shifts.

## Tone shift: horror → comedy

LOTI's signature move is suspenseful horror scoring under the build-up, dead
silence on the freeze frame, then upbeat cartoon payoff. Study:
- **Courage the Cowardly Dog** materials (in CN leak) — the master of horror-comedy tone
- **Billy & Mandy** — dark comedy timing
