# Production Conversation — In the Bushes Character Concept Art

**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Show:** In the Bushes
**Status:** Active character-development reference

## New showrunner-supplied references

The showrunner supplied character sketches directly in the production conversation.

### Busch
The supplied sketch establishes Busch as a small, irregular green bush with an expressive face and bright red shoes. The foliage is loose and organic, with a handmade/crayon-like outline. The eyes are large and expressive and the facial construction is deliberately simple.

**Production implications:**
- Preserve the irregular leafy silhouette.
- Keep Busch visibly plant-like rather than converting him into a generic rounded mascot.
- Treat the red shoes as a defining visual accent.
- Let foliage deformation, squash/stretch, eye direction, and branch/leaf movement carry acting.
- Preserve some handmade energy in the cleaned design.

### Mr. Gold
The supplied sketch establishes Mr. Gold as a separate purple character with a large red hat, simple white limbs, an oversized rounded body, expressive eyes, and a broad toothy grin. The drawing is intentionally rough and exaggerated.

**Production implications:**
- Keep the purple body and red hat as the primary silhouette identifiers.
- Use the simple limbs economically while giving the head/body strong acting poses.
- Preserve exaggerated/asymmetric facial expression.
- Do not merge Mr. Gold into the Busch rig; he is a separate character.

## Canon/provenance rule

These sketches are showrunner-supplied concept references. They are authoritative for visual direction but are not automatically the final animation assets. Cleaned/vector/rigged assets must remain separate from source references and must not be mislabeled as the originals.

## Current build decision

The existing procedural teen system remains the opening cast pipeline. Busch continues to use his reusable character assets. Mr. Gold is recorded for a future recurring-character build rather than being inserted into EP01 without a story reason.

## Next character-asset work

1. Refine Busch production model toward the supplied silhouette while retaining reusable rig components.
2. Add Busch shoe/foot treatment to the production model and motion library.
3. Build a dedicated Mr. Gold model sheet and lightweight cutout rig when his first scene is scheduled.
4. Keep source concept art, cleaned production art, and rendered episode assets in separate provenance layers.
