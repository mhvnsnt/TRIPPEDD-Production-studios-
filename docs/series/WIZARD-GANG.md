## Series premise

The Wizard Gang is the robed inner circle of AshLane's street power — the secret council behind Onyx's gang in the Hollows district. Eight members in locked robe colors, plus an occasional ninth seat that is sometimes empty and sometimes is not.

The council is inspired by Shadow Wizard Money Gang iconography — hooded robes, void-black faces, gold chains — but **it is NEVER called "Shadow Wizard Money Gang" in-game. The in-game proprietary name is TBD.** In these production documents the series/segment is titled **"Wizard Gang"** per the owner's phrasing (owner-approved-in-principle 2026-10-07); the in-game name stays TBD until he locks it.

The series is developed **simultaneously as a short, a segment, and a series**: the same ~50-second production is (a) a standalone short, (b) a recurring segment embedded in TRIPPEDD show episodes, and (c) the pilot material of the standalone Wizard Gang series. This is the first property launched under the studio's development rule that ships all three functions from inception.

## Core influences

The creative starting points include:

- The **El Toro de Oro entrance cinematic** (repo reference look: dark arena, controlled spotlight, smoke, pyro, deliberate camera cuts, character-focused presentation) — this is the production model for every Wizard Gang short, per the owner's promo-video law.
- SWMG crew aesthetics — hooded robe lineups, gold-chain energy, hip-hop jewelry over robes, diamond-grill imagery, direct-address poses.
- The Hollows district's "wizards of the street" visual language: hooded rooftop silhouettes backlit in purple, oversized gold-chain architectural motifs, diamond-grill mouth murals (menacing, not cartoonish), green/purple cauldron-like gathering fires, robes over streetwear for grunts, glowing-eye wall motifs with "IT SEES" tags, spellbook-style graffiti with street slang as "spells", double-cup murals.
- Malakor palette: pitch black × neon purple (`#9d4edd`) / deep blue / toxic green + gold (HOLLOWS_DISTRICT.md lines 37–42). Modern high fidelity — never low-poly, never PS1, never retro (MALAKOR.md line 33).

These are **mechanism and mood references**, not instructions to copy another property's surface style.

## The Wizard rule

> **The council does not explain itself. It declares.**

Dialogue is sparse. Speech lands like oaths, roll-calls, and title cards. The audience learns the council the way the street learns it: by seeing the colors, hearing the names, and feeling the weight — never by exposition dump.

A Wizard Gang beat should be understandable through some combination of:

- robe color and silhouette (color-blocked identity)
- gold-chain and pendant iconography
- direct-address gaze (two tiny eye glints in the dark)
- gathering-fire light and smoke
- ritual movement (entrances, seatings, circles)
- graffiti and wall tags ("IT SEES")
- sound (chains, fire, sub-bass, the city at night)
- title cards with locked canon text only

## Camera grammar — the El Toro de Oro law

The owner's promo-video law applies to every Wizard Gang short: **staged entrance cinematics** — multiple camera angles, lighting changes, working character animation, title cards, original/licensed-safe audio. NOT plain gameplay recordings with music, NO fake gameplay, NO HUD recordings.

Default shot grammar (from UNIVERSAL_ENTRANCE_VIDEO_KIT.md reference look + "Luck of the Irish" staging in EP01-THE-WALK-CANON.md):

`ENVIRONMENT → FIGURE → DETAIL → MOVEMENT → INTERRUPTION → DIRECT ADDRESS → CONSEQUENCE → TITLE CARD`

- Shot A — far/wide, slow push, suspense (rooftop silhouettes, cauldron fire)
- Shot B — different angle, closer, suspense (robe color reveals, chain glints)
- Shot C — back to wide or slow orbit, character in full presence
- The camera moves deliberately. Character motion comes from **real mocap** (see MOCAP_PLAN.md), never procedural bone-wiggling.

## The Narrator rule

The Narrator is the owner's avatar (purple robe, void-black face, sparkly eye glints, diamond-grill smile, blonde braids, gold chains — art-direction/swmg-malakor.md line 202).

- He exists **OUTSIDE the fiction**. He talks to the PLAYER, never to the characters.
- He breaks the 4th wall **ONLY at story-progression moments** — never constant commentary.
- **Never mix the Narrator and Ashes.** They are separate entities with separate robes (purple vs scarlet).
- His voice identity is the owner's own voice — pending (tools/voice/REFERENCE_LEDGER.md lines 19–21). Until he records, the Narrator appears in direct-address visual beats **without spoken lines** — never placeholder voice, never synthetic filler.
- In SHORT 01, the Narrator's cameo is the sanctioned story-progression beat: the council's public reveal to the viewer. That reveal IS the story-progression moment.

## Sound and music direction

Per the owner's music direction (2026-10-06): intro/promo music does **not** have to be fully synthesized in code — pull in open-source samples, loops, and mixing tools and combine with code synthesis (hybrid approach).

- Soundscape: fire crackle, chains clinking, city-night room tone, sub-bass hits, slow ritual percussion.

## Format spec — the Wizard Gang short

There was no ~50-second format spec in the repo (the standing short contract is the 10–20s network ident). This spec is authored here from the ident contract + the entrance-kit evidence gates + the owner's standing ~50s promo length:

- **Runtime:** ~50 seconds.
- **Aspect ratios:** 9:16 primary (shorts/distribution), 16:9 secondary, 1:1 for social cards. Thumbnail/still per shot.
- **Structure:** cold open on the world (Hollows night) → council reveal sequence (robe colors one by one) → Narrator direct-address → "WIZARD GANG" title card → TRIPPEDD network ident card.
- **Title cards:** canon-locked text only ("WIZARD GANG", "TRIPPEDD"). No placeholder, no "not in canon" text.
- **Evidence gates (from UNIVERSAL_ENTRANCE_VIDEO_KIT.md):** the intended game/build was actually rendered; intended character assets actually loaded at runtime; rig/skin/deformation evidence clean; every move/effect shown exists in that build or is explicitly marked production-only; no other game's branding/UI/arena/character data leaked; no placeholder canon data; audio checked; source build, model, timeline, and output hashes recorded. UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN.
- **Provenance:** every generated or transformed asset retains generation method, source/reference assets, software/tool, version, prompt or recipe when available, and editorial purpose.
- **Delivery:** MP4 + provenance manifest + SHA-256 checksums per the pipeline `OPEN → IDENTIFY GAME → VERIFY ASSET → STAGE → TIMELINE → CAPTURE → QA → EXPORT → HASH → REVIEW`.
