# In the Bushes — EP01 Origin Opening v3: Alley / Police Cold Open

## Target
37 seconds at 24 fps. This pass supersedes the earlier generic-night opening. The alley is now the actual origin location and the teenagers must physically run **out of the alley** before throwing the beer into the bush.

## Beat 1 — Hanging out where they should not be (0:00–0:03.5)
Wide nighttime alley. Three teenagers hang out with a six-pack and opened cans. Brick walls, fire escape, dumpster, puddles, alley lamp and the distant street opening give the scene a real geography.

**Movement:** small conversational gestures, can handoff, foot tap, distant environmental motion. Keep it alive without making it busy.

## Beat 2 — Police arrive (0:03.5–0:06)
A distant siren is heard first. Headlights and an abstract police-light sweep begin reflecting down the alley. Footsteps approach. The teens notice the light before we need to show a detailed police character.

**Comedy:** the audience understands the problem a half-second before the teens fully react.

## Beat 3 — “Shit! They're coming!” (0:06–0:09)
One teen spots the approaching police presence and breaks the casual mood: **“Shit! They're coming!”** The group grabs the beer and scrambles toward the exit.

## Beat 4 — Run and duck (0:09–0:12.5)
The teenagers run down the alley and duck near the exit behind the dumpster/wall. They peek back toward the police presence. Siren, footsteps and light continue behind them.

**Important geography lock:** they are hiding inside the alley. The bush is visible farther ahead at the alley exit. They have not thrown the beer yet.

## Beat 5 — “We still have the beer” problem (0:12.5–0:15.5)
They catch their breath. A can rattles. Someone looks down and realizes they are still carrying the six-pack.

**Movement:** can rattle, handoff, nervous glance backward, then a glance toward the bush.

## Beat 6 — “What do we do with the beer?” (0:15.5–0:17.5)
A teen asks: **“What do we do with the beer?”** Hold the silence just long enough for everyone to look toward the bush.

## Beat 7 — Title phrase (0:17.5–0:19)
One teen points toward the bush and shouts: **“IN THE BUSHES!”**

This remains the first spoken occurrence of the show's title. The phrase happens while they are still in/at the alley and **before** the throw.

## Beat 8 — Run OUT and throw (0:19–0:22.5)
The teens burst out of the alley toward the bush. Only after the shouted phrase do they throw the six-pack and loose cans. One pack arcs, cans spin, one clips foliage, and an opened can spills across the bush.

**Critical staging:** the throw happens outside the alley at/near the bush. Do not stage the throw while they are still hiding inside the alley.

## Beat 9 — Beer changes the bush (0:22.5–0:27)
The teenagers continue fleeing. Beer drips through the leaves. One twitch. Another. Then an unnatural shudder and supernatural pulse. The movement becomes intentional rather than wind-driven.

**Transformation language:** surreal, comic-book energy, but original to In the Bushes. Beer is the cause and part of the show's mythology.

## Beat 10 — Busch wakes (0:27–0:30)
Busch rises into a readable conscious pose. Eyes register confusion and a slightly drunk awareness.

## Beat 11 — Busch processes the scene (0:30–0:33)
Busch scans the alley exit, notices the teenagers fleeing, then looks down at the spilled cans. He has just become alive and has absolutely no context.

## Beat 12 — Look-away (0:33–0:35)
Busch deliberately looks away. The beat should communicate: “I don't know what I just witnessed, and I'm not getting involved.”

This remains a distinct comedy beat and must not be merged with the title transition.

## Beat 13 — Reaction hold → title (0:35–0:37)
Tiny sway/blink and an awkward hold. Then hard cut to **IN THE BUSHES**. Busch remains subtly visible behind/around the lettering.

### Animation budget
Use reusable teen states, Busch pose swaps, beer-object translations/rotations, police-light sweeps, camera movement, holds, and a small number of transformation effects. The extra runtime exists to make the origin feel like a real scene, not a longer slideshow.

### Continuity lock
**ALLEY HANGOUT → POLICE ARRIVE → “SHIT! THEY'RE COMING!” → RUN/DUCK/HIDE → REALIZE THEY HAVE BEER → “WHAT DO WE DO WITH THE BEER?” → “IN THE BUSHES!” → RUN OUT OF ALLEY → THROW → TRANSFORMATION → BUSCH WAKES → PROCESSES → LOOKS AWAY → REACTION HOLD → TITLE.**
