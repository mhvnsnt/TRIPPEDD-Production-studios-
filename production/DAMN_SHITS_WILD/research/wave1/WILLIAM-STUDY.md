## Relationship to trying hard

He doesn't. That's the character. Every episode engine for Aaron should start from: *he wants to do the least, and the world rewards him for it anyway* — which horrifies him.

## How he handles being the center of attention

Poorly and passively. He doesn't fight it (that would be effort); he endures it with one-liners. The attention always misreads him: silence becomes wisdom, apathy becomes cool, a throwaway line becomes philosophy.
