# DAMN, SHIT'S WILD — Show Bible

**Title:** "Damn, Shit's Wild" (owner-approved 2026-10-09 — Curb-style sentence title from Aaron Ruben's signature saying).
**Network:** TRIPPEDD NETWORK.
**Status:** BIBLE v1 — owner-written facts + authorized William study. EP01 pilot written, awaiting owner read.

## Logline

A deadpan regular guy keeps getting pulled into situations he wants no part of, tries to exit with minimum effort, and somehow becomes the main character every time.

## The character — Aaron Ruben

- The CHARACTER name is Aaron Ruben. The real person he's based on is William Boydenheimer Carlin (comedian friend — no likeness/voice cloning without separate approval).
- **Voice (from the authorized William message study, ~48 messages, 2026-10-09):** extremely terse, low-volume, mostly lowercase energy, delayed one-line replies. Laid-back bro/dude warmth. Profanity casual, never aggressive. Dry understatement ("Neat."). Deadpan deflection. Absurd self-deprecation when asked for advice. Emoji sequences used as the punchline. Minimalist approval. Funny because he refuses to try hard or carry the emotional conversation.
- **Signature lines:** "damn, shit's wild." / "Neat." / "Nice. It's coming together."
- **Function:** the familiar oddball coworker/neighbor/regular — not a hero. Clerks/Silent Bob energy, Randy (Trailer Park Boys), Workaholics coworkers, Tom Anderson (Beavis and Butt-Head), Bill (King of the Hill), Carl (Aqua Teen).
- **Engine:** social-awareness missteps + self-sabotaging loser energy. Mundanely absurd. Never gets the girl. Not a sex-joke guy. Never punches down.

## Tone & comedy model

Louis CK / Curb Your Enthusiasm / Seinfeld: mundane situations, no laugh track, comedy from character and misstep rather than jokes-per-page. Everyday irritation → half-hearted attempt to fix it → every attempt makes it worse → ends trapped in a new, slightly worse status quo. No lessons learned. Nobody grows.

## Art direction

- **Primary: 2D.** Archer / Assy McGee / Simpsons / Family Guy blend (owner 2026-10-09).
- **Rare 3D:** models of Aaron exist but 3D is used ONLY when the models work perfectly.
- **The 3D device (owner 2026-10-09):** when a 3D segment happens and Aaron is in 3D, **he feels weird about it.** He notices the dimension shift, says so deadpan, and wants to go back to 2D. The medium change is diegetic — he's aware of it and uncomfortable. Never played as cool; always played as wrong.

## Episode structure (Curb model)

1. **Cold open:** a small mundane irritation.
2. **The tangle:** Aaron tries the lowest-effort exit. It fails.
3. **Escalation:** each attempt to disengage pulls him deeper. Other characters misread his deadpan as wisdom, leadership, or menace.
4. **The trap:** he ends up responsible for the thing he was avoiding.
5. **Button:** one deadpan line. No resolution. ("damn. shit's wild.")

## The NOT list (owner — hard)

No improv "yes and." No stand-up voice. No sitcom multi-cam. No laugh track. No reality-TV confessionals. No celebrity parodies. No music videos. No sketches with announced premises. No catchphrase comedy (his signature lines are character, not catchphrases — used sparingly). No physical comedy unless failing. No wigs, no costumes, no characters.

## Voice

William's delivery per the voice-ref analysis: dry, deliberate, conversational, high pause ratio — the pauses ARE the performance. Temp VO direction: underplay everything; never punch a line. No voice clone built or approved yet.

## Series devices

- **The 3D weirdness** (above) — recurring.
- **The group chat** — a recurring location/antagonist (see EP01).
- **Misread deadpan** — people keep promoting him for things he doesn't want because his one-liners scan as profound.
- **The refusal to try** — his superpower and his curse.

## What this bible does NOT contain

Future episode beats, season arcs, supporting cast beyond what the owner names. Those get added when he gives them — nothing invented.
