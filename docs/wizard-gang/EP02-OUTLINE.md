## 1. How this outline is built (method, not content)

Applied from `mhvnsnt/samurai-jack-repo` `craft-for-our-shows/CRAFT-EP01.md` +
`method/tartakovsky-method.md`. The **method** is borrowed; Mochiichao's story and
content are not — nothing from that episode appears here.

| Method point | How it lands on EP02 |
|---|---|
| **Environment gets real minutes, first** | The locked 0:00–0:50 intro (50 s, no dialogue) is the cold open. It is byte-locked — do not touch, do not recut. The episode's world is established before anyone speaks. |
| **One sense is the episode's channel: sound** | EP02 is sound-led by the evidence: only 111.39 s of 344.81 s carries dialogue; 12 of 20 scenes have **zero** dialogue lines. The scene beds, BGM, ambient and 47 one-shots carry the episode where voices do not. |
| **Rhythm, not coverage** | Fast-fast-fast-slow. The middle tour (s14–s26) runs on short beds (6–10 s each); the long beds are the slow movements: s13 Derail (32 s), s27 Adjuction (25 s), s12 Order of Business (20 s), s28 Photo Button (20 s). Cut on rhythm, not on information. |
| **Creature / character acting = pose and eyes, not gesture** | No rigs, visemes, or lipsync data exist for EP02 (see §7). When rigs are built, eye-line and posture beats come before extra motion — a character *watching* is a complete beat in this grammar. |
| **Color keys for light states** | No color keys exist for EP02. When backgrounds are rebuilt, each light state gets one graded master key and every composite in that state matches it — this is a GAP, not a decision made here. |
| **Board-first discipline** | No storyboard exists for EP02. Animation does not start until boards + a locked audio cut exist. The audio cut already exists (92-clip timeline, §5); the boards are owed for every scene (§3, §7). |
