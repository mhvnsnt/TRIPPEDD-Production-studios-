## Bible architecture

**Read:** `~/workspace/animation-techniques/steal-like-artist/cn-gigaleak/study-notes/bible-structure-analysis.md`

1. Series overview (the one-page sell)
2. Character applications (standardized questionnaires per character)
3. World / environmental parameters
4. Story premises (8-12 to prove the concept)
5. Production notes
