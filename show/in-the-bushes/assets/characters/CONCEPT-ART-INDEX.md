# In the Bushes — Character Concept Art Index

**Source:** user-supplied original sketches in the production conversation.
**Received:** 2026-09-15
**Purpose:** preserve the character-development reference and connect rough concept art to production-ready assets.

## 01 — Busch

**Reference:** user sketch showing the green bush character with wide eyes, organic foliage, a small mouth/nose, and red shoes.

### Direction captured
- Busch is an actual living bush character, not a human-shaped mascot.
- The foliage silhouette should stay irregular, leafy, and handmade-looking.
- The face is simple and highly readable: oversized eyes and a small mouth/nose area.
- Red shoes are a defining character accent and should be treated as canon unless the showrunner changes them.
- The roughness of the sketch is part of the personality; production cleanup should preserve the loose, funny silhouette rather than turning Busch into a generic polished tree.
- Animation should exaggerate the foliage itself during wake/transformation/reaction beats.

### Production translation
The existing Busch rig/model sheet remains the animation source. This concept reference is a design-direction source, not a replacement for the reusable production rig.

## 02 — Mr. Gold

**Reference:** user sketch showing a purple character with a large red hat, broad rounded body, simple white line arms/legs, expressive eyes, a wide toothy smile, and a small gold/tan object/detail on the hat and in the mouth area.

### Direction captured
- Character identity is built from a strong purple silhouette.
- Large red hat is the dominant accessory and should remain visually distinctive.
- Face should favor exaggerated asymmetry and simple readable expressions.
- Limbs can remain intentionally loose/simple while the torso/head silhouette carries the character.
- The overall look is intentionally handmade and comedic rather than polished realism.
- Mr. Gold is a separate character from Busch and should not be folded into the Busch rig.

### Production status
Mr. Gold is recorded as a character concept reference. A production rig/model sheet should be created separately when the character enters an episode or recurring-cast build.

## Asset handling

The conversation contains the original uploaded image files. The GitHub production source should use a preserved original/reference copy when binary asset upload is available, plus cleaned/vector production assets separately.

Do **not** replace the original concept reference with a cleaned vector and call the vector the original. Keep source/reference art and production art as separate provenance layers.
