# EP01 Ending — Lost Acid

## Status
**CANONICAL STORY BEAT / PILOT ENDING CANDIDATE**

This document records the proposed ending for the TRIPPEDD pilot. It closes the pilot's existing shroomified storyline without changing the physical-source record.

## Core premise
After the motel hangout, the shroomified attempt has failed to produce the expected trip. The group has gone through the day's escalating events, including leaving to get cigars and returning toward the motel. The protagonist then remembers that he bought acid earlier and realizes he might finally have a way to salvage the day.

He says, in essence: **"Oh yeah, I have the acid."**

He goes to take it / retrieve it — and discovers that he cannot find it.

The acid is simply gone.

## Beat-by-beat

1. **False salvation** — The shroomified plan has not worked as expected. The day appears to have produced everything except the actual trip.
2. **Memory spark** — While returning toward the motel, he suddenly remembers the acid he bought.
3. **Hope** — He becomes briefly excited because this is the obvious solution to the day's failed plan.
4. **Search** — He checks where he believes he put it. Then another place. Then another. The search becomes increasingly frantic and stupidly methodical.
5. **Impossible absence** — It is nowhere. The joke is not that someone dramatically steals it; the joke is that it has apparently ceased to exist.
6. **Deflation** — The frantic energy collapses into genuine, pathetic disappointment.
7. **Button** — He accepts that he is not going to trip today. After everything that happened, the grand endpoint is simply: he lost the acid.
8. **End** — Hold on the sadness/defeat long enough for the anticlimax to become the punchline. Cut out rather than resolving the day with another escalation.

## Tone
The ending should feel **sad, absurd, mundane, and disproportionately devastating**. The comedy comes from the enormous buildup and chaotic day ending in an extremely small personal tragedy.

Do not make the missing acid into a mystery that must be solved. Its disappearance is the button.

Do not require a psychedelic visual sequence to make the ending work. The pilot can end precisely because the expected trip never happens.

## Relationship to the existing pilot
This closes the previously open-ended shroomified thread. The earlier idea that the shroomified effect might take effect while walking back from getting cigars remains a usable alternate/preceding beat, but this ending provides a stronger terminal beat if the trip still does not meaningfully happen.

Recommended format: `LIVE_SKETCH` or `HYBRID` depending on whether the final button is performed from existing footage, newly captured material, or generated/reconstructed material.

Recommended episode grammar placement:
`REALITY_ANCHOR → ESCALATION → FORMAT_SHIFT → CALLBACK → PRESSURE_COOKER → [LOST ACID BUTTON]`

The lost-acid beat is the **terminal anticlimax**: the story does not need another payoff after it.
