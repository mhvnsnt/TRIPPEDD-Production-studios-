## Delivery shape

| Register | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Whisper** | Sibilant, close-mic hiss; elongated sibilants ("knowsss", "cuttsssss"); barely intelligible | "he sees you..." (entity address) |
| **Gabble** | Fast, staccato word-loops; near-unintelligible patter | "bet bet bet, I bet I can do it" / "obsessed... black... obsessed... black..." |
| **Shriek** | Sudden loud peaks out of the whisper; the catchphrase shape | "The Rain! The rain...cuts...deep!" (shout → decay) |
| **Manic laugh** | Delighted, unhinged; pain reads as pleasure | Fail beat: grinning while hurt |

## Signature tics (all sourced)

1. **Third-person entity** — talks about/to "he"/"Him" (never named): "He knows!", "he is listening", "He sees you." (WhatCulture, March 2026; wrestlinginc, June 2026)
2. **First-person plural** — "WE are obsessed" (his own X post, via sportskeeda)
3. **Single-word loops** — repeats one word until it degrades: "obsessed... black..." through the TMZ interview until the clip faded (sportskeeda)
4. **"No no no" denial loops** — "No no no... he sees you" (bannon_dialogue.json: AMBUSH_BACKSTAGE, PRE_MATCH_LOCKER_ROOM)
5. **"Cuts" escalation** — "the cuts, the cuts, the cuts! Stronger and stronger and stronger!" (bannon_dialogue.json)
6. **Rain profession** — "The Rain! The rain...cuts...deep!" with umbrella (wrestlinginc, June 2026); "the rain is coming, and it washes the weak away" (bannon_dialogue.json: TAG_TEAM_BETRAYAL)

## Zoned-out vs manic

- **Zoned out:** thousand-yard stare, whisper register, slow sibilant delivery, talks PAST people to "him." Body still or swaying.
- **Manic:** wide white-contact stare, grin spreading, gabble → shriek, clutching head, crawling, erratic movement. The shift between the two is sudden — that's the comedy and the horror.

## Comedic beats

The comedy comes from the CONTRAST: utter conviction in nonsense ("He sees you taping your wrists. You think that tape holds you together?"), delight in pain ("Aaaw man, he knowsss..."), and the loop-till-it-breaks patter. Never winks at the audience — he is completely serious, which is what makes it funny.

## EP01 line direction

**C1 — the bet** ("I bet I can. Bet bet bet, I bet I can do it, I know I can. He knows. He sees you. The rain!"):
Starts fast gabbling (bet-bet-bet loop), drops quieter and certain for the entity address ("He knows. He sees you"), then SHRIEKS "The rain!" like the catchphrase.

**C2 — the fail** ("Aaaw man, he knowsss... he knowsss. Cuts... cuttsssss."):
Pained but grinning — the "aaaw man" is almost delighted. Sibilants stretched, trailing into hiss. Grabs his knee, smiles maniacally: the pain feeds "him."
