## ACT 1 — THE SUMMONS / THE MEETING (0:50–2:10)

### S10 — "THE SUMMONS" (0:50–1:02, 12s)
- **CAM:** Match-cut from the cold open's rooftop fire — slow push on the flare, then whip-pan across the skyline.
- **LIGHT:** Green cauldron flare, night.
- **ACT:** The gathering fire flares — the council's call. Eight robed figures look up from across the Hollows. The 9th seat's fire stays dark. (Kiko is late. Again.)
- **ASSET:** `cartoonier/Group 3 - rooftop.webp` (base) + `detailed/Painterly group 3 - storm rooftop.webp` (flare beat, dramatic).
- **AUD:** Sub-bass hit, wind, chains. Narrator VO (VO-PENDING): **N1** — "They got the call. All nine."
- **SETUP:** the summons; Kiko's empty fire (pays off at S26/S28).

### S11 — "THE MEETING — solemn opening" (1:02–1:18, 16s)
- **CAM:** Slow push across the summit table; pan from the 5 unrobed street crew to the 9 robed wizards.
- **LIGHT:** Candlelight + string lights, night warehouse.
- **ACT:** Onyx raises one hand for order. The ritual candles are lit (`Painterly group 2 - ritual.webp` beat). Static talks THROUGH the entire ritual — lines L2–L3. Nobody stops him; the candle gutters out on its own.
- **THE WINK (1:12):** Static squints at the street crew, then at the matching robes — line L4 ("does everybody at this table look REAL familiar? …Without the bathrobes. Nah. Forget it.") — then the camera cuts away. Never stated. Never explained.
- **ASSET:** `detailed/Cross-faction 1 - summit (1).webp` — THE single sanctioned summit appearance (cartoonier treatment per owner direction) + `detailed/Painterly group 2 - ritual.webp` (failed-ritual beat).
- **AUD:** Ritual drum under Static's yap; drum dies when the candle dies.
- **SETUP:** the meeting's solemnity (pays off: nothing solemn survives the episode).

### S12 — "ORDER OF BUSINESS" (1:18–1:38, 20s)
- **CAM:** Roll-call lineup; headcount pan.
- **LIGHT:** Same room.
- **ACT:** Static runs the roll call (line L5). Eight names land. The 9th doesn't. "Somebody tell Kiko the meetin' MOVED, he's at the wrong pier again!" Ashes stands — DECLARES (line H2, HELD — staged visual-only in Ep1; Static quotes him in L6: "He said we move tonight!"). The war fund is pooled — a hat full of cash. Cipher's hand drifts toward the dice in his pocket (line L7).
- **ASSET:** `detailed/Cross-faction 1 - summit (1).webp` (second angle/crop of the table) + `cartoonier/SWMG group - cartoon 2.webp` (roll-call lineup).
- **RUNNING GAG PLANTED:** the group-cartoon lineup is missing Hollow (orange) — he is nowhere in the frame. Nobody mentions it. (Pays off S28.)
- **SETUPS:** the war fund (pays off S14/S18/S22); Ashes' declaration "we move tonight" (pays off: nobody moves — S28 button).

### S13 — "THE DERAIL BEGINS" (1:38–2:10, 32s)
- **CAM:** Smash cut — the meeting table is now beside a lit grill. Slow orbit of the BBQ.
- **LIGHT:** Firelight, dusk-to-night.
- **ACT:** Somebody fired up the grill mid-meeting. The war-plan map is already a placemat. Theory — the only one taking notes — is drawing the grill (payoff at S27; visual, no line — her speaking style is UNKNOWN). Sombra checks his watch (line H3, HELD: "My rate is hourly. This is hour three."). Onyx takes a burger without a word. The summit has become a cookout.
- **ASSET:** `cartoonier/Group 13 - rooftop BBQ.webp` — the Act 2 hub; the episode keeps returning here between beats.
- **AUD:** Sizzle, laughter, Static mid-yap (line L6 tail).
- **TRANSITION:** smash-cut montage begins → Act 2.
