# Wizard Gang Character Rigs — v1 Concept Drafts

Preston Blair-spec model sheet sets for all 8 wizards (Ashes has his own pack at `~/workspace/ashes-mouth-pack/`).

## Status: CONCEPT DRAFTS

These are first-draft explorations. The art style is NOT the show's locked style — it's a variant the owner likes and has approved for potential episode use.

## Art Style Rule (owner 2026-10-10)

- Art style MAY vary slightly from the show's base look.
- **Max 2 art styles per episode.** Pick the styles upfront, stick to them.
- **Do not deviate** from the chosen style(s) once an episode starts.
- **Likeness stays locked** through the whole episode regardless of style — same character design, same features, same identity.

## Contents per character

Each folder has:
- `visemes/` — 10 Preston Blair mouth position cards (pixel-aligned from single base)
- `eyes/` — 6 eye expression cards
- `hands/` — 8-10 hand pose cards
- `*-MODEL-SHEET.md` — filled model sheet
- `*-ANIMATION-GUIDE.md` — animation notes
