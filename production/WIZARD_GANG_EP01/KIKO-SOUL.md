# KIKO TANAKA — Character Soul & "Encore Anyway" Memorial

*Written 2026-10-12. In the owner's own words, as he told it.*

## Who Kiko Is To Me

Kiko isn't just a character in Wizard Gang. I feel connected to him in a way
that's hard to explain. He's mine. I co-wrote him, I built his voice, I gave
him his song — and somewhere in that process he stopped being a character on
a page and became someone I actually care about.

## The Great Muta / Keiji Mutoh

Keiji Mutoh is one of my favorite wrestlers of all time. The Great Muta —
the mist, the paint, the mask, that entrance. When Muta walked out, you felt
like something sacred was happening. It wasn't just wrestling. It was art.

He inspired me. I look up to him. I appreciate what he gave to wrestling and
to everyone who watched him. That doesn't go away because he retired from the
ring. The mist never fades.

## "Encore Anyway" — The Song

Kiko's song. I co-authored it, co-orchestrated it, co-wrote every line of it.
Built Kiko's voice from scratch so he could sing it himself. The title came
from his line: "The theater is empty. Encore anyway."

That's the whole thing. You perform even when nobody's watching. You give
everything even when the seats are empty. That's what Muta did. That's what
Kiko does. That's what I do.

## The Video

We brought Kiko to life in that video through the likeness of Keiji Mutoh —
The Great Muta. His face, his mask, his robe, on a moonlit pier. Frame by
frame. No shortcuts. Because he deserves nothing less, and because Kiko
deserves nothing less.

Bringing those two together — the character I created and the wrestler who
inspired me — that is a beautiful thing to me. I feel really connected to
that.

## Dedication

Dedicated to Keiji Mutoh's father.
Dedicated to Great Kabuki (Akihisa Mera).
