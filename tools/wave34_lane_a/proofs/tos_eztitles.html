Secure Media Access & Control

EVS – EZTitles Video Sharing

Where sensitive media stays protected and accessible

EVS is the secure media access and control layer of the EZTitles ecosystem. It allows authorized editors to stream sensitive video directly inside EZTitles while organizations retain control over where the media is stored, who can access it and which protections apply to every session.

  • Encrypted video streaming
  • Dynamic per-user watermarking
  • Screen-recording protection
  • MFA-protected authentication
  • Role-based media access
  • Customer-controlled or managed storage options

EVS does not replace a full Media Asset Management system. It provides the secure video preparation, storage access and streaming controls required for professional subtitle production.
