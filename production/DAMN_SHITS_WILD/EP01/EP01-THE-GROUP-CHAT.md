# DAMN, SHIT'S WILD — EP01: "The Group Chat"

**Format:** 2D animated (Archer/Assy McGee/Simpsons/Family Guy blend). One 3D "dramatic reenactment" device — Aaron feels weird about it.
**Status:** DRAFT — owner read/approval required before voice or boards. Nothing here is locked until he says so.

---

## COLD OPEN

**INT. AARON'S APARTMENT — NIGHT**

AARON RUBEN (30s, permanent just-woke-up hair, t-shirt from a comedy show that ended in 2019) sits on a couch that has given up. A half-eaten sandwich. His phone BUZZES. Then BUZZES again. Then becomes a small angry insect.

**AARON** (not looking up)
No.

The phone keeps buzzing. He looks. A notification:

> **MAPLE ST NEIGHBORHOOD WATCH 🏘️**
> Linda added you to the group. 47 members.

**AARON**
(beat)
Neat.

He puts the phone face-down. It buzzes through the couch cushion like it's trying to reach him personally.

**TITLE CARD: DAMN, SHIT'S WILD** — wavy trippy, white on black.

---

## ACT ONE

**INT. AARON'S APARTMENT — CONTINUOUS**

Aaron picks the phone back up. The chat is already moving fast.

> **LINDA:** Welcome Aaron!! 🏘️🎉 We're so glad to have you!!
> **LINDA:** This is our neighborhood family! Please introduce yourself!
> **MARCUS:** welcome bro
> **DEB:** Hi Aaron!! What street are you on??

Aaron stares at the screen the way you'd stare at a parking ticket.

**AARON** (typing, deleting, typing)
hey

He hits send. Three dots appear instantly.

> **LINDA:** That's it?? 😂 Tell us about yourself!!
> **DEB:** Are you the Aaron with the blue car or the Aaron with the dog??

**AARON** (to himself)
There's two Aarons.

He types:

> **AARON:** blue car i think

> **LINDA:** The blue car Aaron!! We love the blue car Aaron!!
> **MARCUS:** blue car go hard tho

**AARON** (to himself, quiet)
damn. shit's wild.

He opens the group settings. Taps "Leave group." A confirmation pops up: *Are you sure?*

**AARON**
Yes. That's the whole thing I'm sure about.

He taps yes. The chat disappears.

He exhales. Picks up the sandwich.

The phone buzzes.

> **MAPLE ST NEIGHBORHOOD WATCH 🏘️**
> Linda added you to the group.

**AARON**
Nope.

He leaves again. Eats. Buzz.

> **MAPLE ST NEIGHBORHOOD WATCH 🏘️**
> Linda added you to the group.

**AARON** (typing)
linda please stop adding me

> **LINDA:** But you're one of us now!! 🏘️❤️
> **DEB:** Linda don't let him leave!!
> **MARCUS:** aaron stay bro

Aaron looks at the ceiling. The ceiling has no answers.

---

## ACT TWO

**INT. AARON'S APARTMENT — LATER**

Aaron has decided on a strategy: be so boring they remove him themselves. He watches the chat.

> **DEB:** Has anyone seen my cat?? Orange, answers to Biscuit, very shy!!
> **DEB:** Please keep an eye out 🙏🙏🙏
> **LINDA:** Oh no!! Everyone please look for Biscuit!!

A long pause. Aaron types the most disengaged thing he can think of:

> **AARON:** hope biscuit's ok

The three dots. Then:

> **DEB:** Thank you Aaron 😭 That means so much
> **LINDA:** See?? This is what community looks like 🏘️❤️
> **MARCUS:** aaron a real one

**AARON** (to himself)
That was six words.

He tries a different tactic — total silence. He mutes the chat. For two days, peace.

**INT. GROCERY STORE — DAY**

Aaron is buying sandwich supplies. His phone buzzes — a DM, not the group. It's LINDA.

> **LINDA:** Hi Aaron!! Just checking you're okay!! You went quiet!!
> **LINDA:** The group misses you!! Biscuit is still missing 😢

Aaron stands in the bread aisle for a long time.

**AARON** (typing)
im ok

> **LINDA:** Can you come to the search party Saturday?? We're meeting at the park!! Everyone's coming!!

**AARON**
I have plans.

> **LINDA:** What plans??

Aaron looks at his cart. Sandwich supplies.

**AARON** (typing, slowly)
sandwich plans

He stares at what he's written. Sends it anyway.

The reply takes ten seconds. When it comes, it's not Linda. It's DEB:

> **DEB:** 😂😂😂

Then MARCUS:

> **MARCUS:** sandwich plans LMAO

Then, somehow, the whole group chat — the one he muted — lights up. Linda has screenshotted his DM and posted it to the group.

> **LINDA:** Our Aaron!! 😂😂 "sandwich plans"!!
> **DEB:** I'm sorry this made my whole week
> **MARCUS:** sandwich plans is crazy 😭

**AARON** (very quiet)
No. No no no.

---

## ACT THREE

**INT. AARON'S APARTMENT — NIGHT**

The group chat has a new energy. Aaron's "sandwich plans" has become a thing. People are using it.

> **MARCUS:** cant come to poker, sandwich plans
> **DEB:** Tell my boss I have sandwich plans 😂

Worse: they've decided he's funny. Worse than that: they've decided he's WISE.

> **LINDA:** Aaron, you always know what to say. What should we do about Biscuit?

Aaron types, deletes, types:

> **AARON:** idk. cats come back when they feel like it. or they don't. that's the whole deal with cats.

The chat goes quiet for a full minute. Then:

> **DEB:** ...that's actually really beautiful
> **LINDA:** 😭😭😭
> **MARCUS:** bro just dropped philosophy in the neighborhood watch

**AARON** (to himself, horrified)
That wasn't philosophy. That was giving up.

His phone rings. It's a number he doesn't know. He answers because something in him has surrendered to the week.

**AARON**
Hello?

**LINDA (V.O.)**
Aaron!! Hi!! So — the group voted. We want you to lead the Biscuit search party on Saturday!!

**AARON**
Linda. I don't want to lead anything. I have never led anything.

**LINDA (V.O.)**
That's exactly why you're perfect!! You're so — grounded!! Everyone agrees!!

**AARON** (long pause)
...Neat.

**LINDA (V.O.)**
Is that a yes?!

**AARON**
That's a "neat."

**LINDA (V.O.)**
We'll take it!! See you Saturday!! Bring sandwich supplies!!

She hangs up. Aaron holds the phone slightly away from his face, like it might explain itself.

## THE 3D DEVICE

**EXT. THE PARK — DAY (3D ANIMATION)**

A title card: **DRAMATIC REENACTMENT**. The style shifts — suddenly everything is 3D. The park is too detailed. The trees have individual leaves. It looks expensive and wrong.

AARON stands in the middle of it in full 3D, holding a sandwich platter. He looks down at his hands. Turns them over.

**AARON**
...I feel weird about this.

A 3D NEIGHBOR jogs past with a "LOST CAT" poster.

**3D NEIGHBOR**
Have you seen Biscuit?!

**AARON** (not moving)
I feel like I'm in a cutscene.

**3D NEIGHBOR**
What?

**AARON**
Nothing. I don't like it here.

He looks directly at the camera.

**AARON**
Can we go back to the regular one.

**SMASH CUT TO 2D.**

**EXT. THE PARK — DAY (2D)**

Aaron stands with the sandwich platter. LINDA approaches with a clipboard.

**LINDA**
Aaron!! You made it!! And you brought sandwiches!!

**AARON**
I brought one sandwich. For me.

**LINDA** (taking it)
The group will love this!!

She walks off with his sandwich. Aaron watches her go.

---

## BUTTON

**EXT. THE PARK — LATER**

The search party found nothing. Biscuit remains at large. The group sits on the grass, deflated. DEB is near tears.

DEB looks at Aaron.

**DEB**
Aaron... say something.

Aaron looks at the group. Forty-seven neighbors waiting on his deadpan like it's scripture. He thinks for a long moment.

**AARON**
...cat's gonna do what the cat's gonna do.

Silence. Then DEB starts laughing — real laughing, the first time all week.

**DEB**
God. You're right.

The group nods. Someone starts clapping. Aaron closes his eyes.

**AARON** (to himself)
damn. shit's wild.

**SMASH TO BLACK.**

**END CARD:** white on black, wavy — **STAY TRIPPEDD**.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Format:** 2D throughout except the marked 3D reenactment. The 3D device is diegetic — Aaron notices and dislikes it.
- **Voice direction:** underplay everything. Aaron never punches a line; the pauses do the work. Temp VO until William's voice situation is resolved (voice-ref on file, no clone approved).
- **Texting:** Aaron's texts render on screen in his voice — lowercase, terse, delayed. The chat UI is a recurring visual.
- **Linda** is the only character allowed to use emojis. Everyone else: minimal.
- **Biscuit** is never found on screen. Whether Biscuit comes home is the owner's call — unresolved is funnier.
- Nothing in this draft is locked. Owner reads first, then voice, then boards.
