# Steal Like An Artist — Goodville
## Study materials for the South Park-style cutout show

> Goodville is a South Park-parody animated cutaway: flat cutout characters,
> crude-by-design, voice-first. These materials teach professional cutout
> structure and pattern design.

## Puppet rig structure (Gumball .fla)

**Read:** `~/workspace/animation-techniques/steal-like-artist/cn-gigaleak/study-notes/fla-rig-conventions.md`

Gumball's rigs are the professional standard for puppet layer hierarchy:
- `GB[code]_[CHARACTER]` layer naming with descriptive sub-layers
- Every body part on its own layer inside the character symbol
- Symbol nesting: Graphic vs MovieClip for mouths/hands/eyes
- Registration points at joints
- 25 FPS timeline, 14:9/6:9 safe guides

**Apply to:** Dickie, Julio, Bubba, Lt. James Dangly puppets. Don't copy
Gumball's shapes — copy the ORGANIZATION.

**Template:** `docs/steal-like-artist-templates/rig-spec-template.md` (copied here)
