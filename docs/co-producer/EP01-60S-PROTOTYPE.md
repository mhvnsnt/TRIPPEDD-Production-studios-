# Co-Producer — EP01 60–90 Second Prototype

## Working Episode Title

**The Easy Way**

## Premise

The protagonist needs to move one small object across a room. The obvious solution takes five seconds. The Instigator insists there is a better way. By the end, four people have created an elaborate system for moving something that could have simply been carried.

## Characters

- **Protagonist:** wants the problem solved quickly.
- **Instigator:** cannot resist improving the solution.
- **Straight-Man:** repeatedly points out the obvious solution.
- **Wild Card:** enthusiastically operates whatever ridiculous system is created.

## Location

One generic room/workplace interior. Keep the background nearly static so the comedy comes from staging and performance.

## Prop

One small box. It should be visually simple enough to redraw, rotate, duplicate, and reuse.

## Shot Plan

| Shot | Time | Action | Production Method |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 01 | 0:00–0:08 | Protagonist notices the box needs to move. | Hold pose + slow push-in |
| 02 | 0:08–0:18 | Protagonist reaches for it. Instigator stops them. | Two reusable poses + mouth swaps |
| 03 | 0:18–0:30 | Instigator unveils a ridiculous "efficient" method. | Prop swap + quick camera push |
| 04 | 0:30–0:44 | Straight-Man says they could just carry it. Nobody listens. | Three-character hold |
| 05 | 0:44–1:00 | Wild Card operates the system; it becomes increasingly unstable. | Reused poses + shake preset + SFX |
| 06 | 1:00–1:12 | Box travels approximately one foot and falls. | Simple object translation + impact |
| 07 | 1:12–1:22 | Protagonist picks up the box and carries it. | Simple walk/arm pose |
| 08 | 1:22–1:30 | Everyone watches in silence. Straight-Man looks at camera. | Hold + reaction + audio button |

## Audio Direction

Keep the track sparse:

- room tone
- dialogue
- a few mechanical clicks
- escalating rattles
- one impact
- silence before the final reaction

The sound design should make the ridiculous machine feel larger than the animation budget.

## Animation Budget

Target fewer than 20 unique character poses for the complete prototype. Most movement should come from:

- camera pushes/pans
- mouth swaps
- arm/hand swaps
- object translation
- two-frame reactions
- shake/impact presets
- holds

## Acceptance Criteria

The prototype is ready when:

- the premise is understandable without exposition;
- the joke escalates logically;
- no shot requires a bespoke full animation cycle unless necessary;
- the same four characters can be reused in the next scene;
- the box and room become reusable production assets;
- dialogue and sound timing make the limited animation feel intentional;
- the final button lands without requiring a long explanation.
