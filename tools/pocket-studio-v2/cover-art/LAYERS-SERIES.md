# MONTREALITY "LAYERS" — Album Cover Designer Interview Series

A MONTREALITY Original Series. Each episode interviews an iconic album cover designer about their creative process. Sponsored by Adobe Photoshop (#AdobePhotoshopPartner).

## Episode 1 — Shawn Brauch (Pen & Pixel)
- **URL:** https://youtu.be/tVz2IAAxttE
- **Who:** Shawn Brauch, co-founder of Pen & Pixel Graphics (Houston, TX)
- **Output:** 19,180+ album covers (1992–2003). House design firm for No Limit Records, Cash Money Records, Suave House Records.
- **Covers discussed:** Master P, Snoop Dogg, Lil Wayne
- **Chapters:** Intro (0:00) / Pen & Pixel origin story (0:45) / Cover Art Breakdown: Master P (2:27) / Tools and Technology (4:08) / Cover Art Breakdown: Snoop Dogg (6:06) / Design Process and Workflow (8:17) / Cover Art Breakdown: Lil Wayne (10:13) / Message to the Youth (11:59)
- **Style:** "More is more." Gaudy, maximalist, diamond-encrusted. Bullets, cars, fire, gems, money, women — wealth iconography composited in Photoshop.
- **Method (from interviews):** Client concept → hand sketch → scan sketch → computer generates a list of needed items (cars, diamonds, jewelry) → photograph real items from multiple angles (reusable asset library) → Photoshop compositing. If the artist didn't want to buy clothes, Pen & Pixel photographed only the face and used body models for the body — "you would never know the difference." Fractional cost of a real photoshoot.
- **Key quote (paraphrased):** Why pay $15–20k for a photoshoot with Bentleys, models, rented jewelry, and locations when Photoshop can do it at one-tenth the price?
- **Legacy:** Invented the visual language of the bling era. Came out of retirement in 2020 for 21 Savage & Metro Boomin's *Savage Mode II* cover (deliberate 90s homage).

## Episode 2 — LONEWOLF (LUCKI's designer)
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BikxAyWIXx4
- **Who:** LONEWOLF (aka whylonewolf), director/designer
- **Style:** Chaotic, paper-cut, mixed-media. Physical collages — cut-and-paste, texture overlays, digital manipulation. Raw and tactile, the opposite of Pen & Pixel's gloss.
- **Covers discussed:** LUCKI — *Days B4 III*, *Dr*gs R Bad*, *Freewave 3*
- **Chapters:** Intro (0:00) / Origin story (0:22) / Mixed Media (1:06) / LUCKI "Days B4 III" (2:43) / The Source of Creativity (4:55) / LUCKI "Dr*gs R Bad" (6:00) / Physical media: Vinyl & CD (8:00) / LUCKI "Freewave 3" (9:06)
- **Method:** Hand-made physical collages scanned and manipulated digitally. Texture is the star — paper grain, torn edges, layered ephemera.

## Why this matters for TRIPPEDD
The owner makes music and fonts. Album cover design is directly in his lane. Pen & Pixel's 90s rap maximalism is the aesthetic target — but built with free tools. LONEWOLF's mixed-media approach is the low-budget parallel path (scissors, paper, phone camera, free editor).
