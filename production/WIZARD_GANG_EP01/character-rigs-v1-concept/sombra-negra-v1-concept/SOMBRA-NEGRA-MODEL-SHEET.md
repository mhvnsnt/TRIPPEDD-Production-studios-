## 8. CHARACTER NOTES

- **RED EYES ARE THE DESIGN.** Never normalize them to brown or "normal." This is the #1 drift hazard on this character.
- **TASSELS ARE THE OUTFIT.** The dark angular shards behind his thighs are loose tassels with their own rigging. Never delete, never "clean up," never call them defects.
- **Face paint vs void face:** This pack uses the WHITE FACE-PAINT MASK + RED EYES head (bible §8 primary head design) with the BLACK ROBE / PURPLE TRIM / SKULL PENDANT (bible §8 primary robe design). Black-void-face variant (robe section) is a documented alternate for robed shadow scenes — flag before mixing both in one episode.
- **Damian Priest likeness:** tall, muscular, deep commanding presence. Skin tone #8E6650 — do NOT whitewash.
- **Pendant:** SKULL pendant, canon-locked. Never "SWMG" text on screen.
- **Mouth/teeth:** white natural teeth, painted white lips — no gold teeth (that's Ashes), no pink lips.
- **Finger count:** 5. Verified on every card.
- **Style:** thick black outlines, flat cel shading, 2D cartoon. NO photorealism, NO Disney 3D, NO AI slop.
