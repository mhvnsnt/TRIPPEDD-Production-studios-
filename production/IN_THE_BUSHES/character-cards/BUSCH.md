# Character Card — BUSCH

**Role:** Lead (established)
**Status:** Confirmed — production model in progress
**Source:** `docs/series/in-the-bushes/character-art/busch concept.png` (owner Drive, 2026-09-12); 2026-09-15 production conversation

## Visual lock

- Small, irregular green bush/blob creature — visibly plant-like, never a generic rounded mascot
- Googly eyes, large and expressive; deliberately simple facial construction
- Bright red shoes — defining visual accent
- Handmade/crayon-like outline, loose organic foliage

## Acting

Foliage deformation, squash/stretch, eye direction, branch/leaf movement.
He stays visibly plant-like in every shot.

## Notes

- Separate rig, separate build. Never merged with any other character.
- Red shoes are non-negotiable in every appearance.
