## Rules this sheet was built under

- Every claim cites `source file + line`. Where canons conflict, the conflict is flagged **per character** — never resolved by invention.
- Where a canon is silent, the slot is marked **UNKNOWN**.
- Nothing invented: no backstories, no personalities, no voice traits beyond what's sourced.
- Comedy framing (owner 2026-10-06, `~/workspace/trippedd-studio/docs/series/WIZARD-GANG.md`, "Series format direction"): King of the Hill / Beavis and Butt-Head / Metalocalypse tone — the 9 wizards try to run the gang wars but constantly lose control partying, drinking, barbecuing, doing bullshit magic. The pilot reveal plays straight; the series plays it as comedy.
- Standing council-voice law: **"The council does not explain itself. It declares."** — `WIZARD-GANG.md` ("The Wizard rule"). Dialogue lands like oaths, roll-calls, title cards — never exposition dumps.
- Standing voice-fidelity rule: **"AI voices must be TIGHTER — closer matches to the real people they're based on"** (owner 2026-10-06) — `WIZARD-GANG.md` voice-cast section, line 158. A character with no ready AI voice does not speak — never a placeholder or wrong-likeness voice (line 160).
- Writers' tuning forks for street dialogue: `BOOK_PARALLELS.md` — *Clockers* ("Read for: dialogue… how people actually talk"), *Down These Mean Streets* (code-switching, "what it costs to wear a persona"), *The Corner* (the block as trap AND home).
