## 8. CHARACTER NOTES

- **Cat-ear headpiece is LAW.** Large pointed blue-violet ears with pink inner ear, worn in unrobed form — signature, NEVER dropped. They never shrink, never flatten, never disappear.
- **Same face across every attire** (EP02 bible S18 rule). The face on the viseme cards is the canon face.
- **Locs are law:** medium-length dark locs past the shoulders with beads/charms (attire 1). They don't lengthen, shorten, or change color between shots.
- **Sunglasses rule:** NO sunglasses on attire 1 or attire 3 (the rejected long-haired sunglasses v1 is DEAD — never regenerate or reuse).
- **Whitewash rule:** Skin is #AE5B2A. If a generation lightens it, reject. Every time.
- **Shroud rule:** In robed form, face is a black void with only eye glints visible. NO visible skin, NO eyebrows, NO mouth on the robed form.
- **Lips are natural warm brown.** Full lips are her feature — never shrink them, never pink them, never gloss them to another ethnicity's lip shape.
- **Septum ring stays.** Small silver ring, centered under the nose. Never lost.
- **Intellectual, not feral:** her emotion reads in precise control — glints that sharpen rather than explode.
- **2D cartoon style locked:** thick black outlines, flat cel shading. NO 3D, NO Pixar, NO Disney, NO photorealism.
