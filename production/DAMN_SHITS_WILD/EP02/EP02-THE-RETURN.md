# DAMN, SHIT'S WILD — EP02: "The Return"

**Format:** 2D animated (Archer/Assy McGee/Simpsons/Family Guy blend).
**Status:** DRAFT — owner read/approval required before voice or boards. Nothing here is locked until he says so.
**Continuity:** Follows EP01 "The Group Chat." Aaron is now reluctantly known as "sandwich plans guy" in certain circles.

---

## COLD OPEN

**INT. AARON'S APARTMENT — MORNING**

AARON stands in his kitchen staring at a TOASTER. One slot is blackened. It smells like a tire fire.

**AARON**
(to the toaster)
You had one job.

He unplugs it. Holds it at arm's length like a dead animal.

**AARON** (to himself)
Returning this. In and out. Nobody talks to anybody.

**TITLE CARD: DAMN, SHIT'S WILD** — wavy trippy, white on black.

---

## ACT ONE

**INT. BIG-BOX STORE — RETURNS DESK — DAY**

The returns line is eight people deep. A sign reads: **RETURNS — PLEASE HAVE RECEIPT READY** 😊.

Aaron does not have a receipt ready. Aaron has a toaster and a feeling.

He waits. The line moves like a glacier with anxiety. Ahead of him, a CUSTOMER is arguing about a blender.

**CUSTOMER**
It blends! It just — it blends wrong!

**CLERK (PAT)**
Sir, "blends wrong" isn't a defect category.

Aaron watches this like it's a nature documentary.

When his turn comes, PAT the clerk gives him the retail smile — the one that's technically a smile.

**PAT**
Receipt?

**AARON**
No.

**PAT**
...Do you have the card you bought it on?

**AARON**
Cash.

**PAT** (typing)
And when did you buy it?

**AARON**
When it worked.

A beat. Pat stares.

**PAT**
I need an approximate date of purchase.

**AARON**
Approximately... before it broke.

Behind Aaron, the line shifts. Someone groans. Aaron feels eight pairs of eyes on the back of his neck and does not turn around, because turning around would be participation.

**PAT**
Sir, without a receipt I can only offer store credit at the current sale price.

**AARON**
How much is the current sale price.

**PAT** (checks)
Nine dollars.

**AARON**
I paid forty.

**PAT**
It's on sale now.

**AARON** (quiet, to himself)
damn. shit's wild.

---

## ACT TWO

**INT. BIG-BOX STORE — RETURNS DESK — CONTINUOUS**

Aaron is doing math out loud, which is always a mistake.

**AARON**
So I bring you a forty-dollar toaster, and you give me nine dollars, but only if I spend it here.

**PAT**
That's the policy.

**AARON**
That's not a return. That's a coupon with extra steps.

The CUSTOMER behind Aaron — a woman in her 40s, DENISE — leans in.

**DENISE**
Are you the sandwich plans guy?

**AARON** (without turning)
No.

**DENISE**
From the Maple Street chat? Linda posted your DM? "Sandwich plans"?

Aaron turns, slowly.

**AARON**
...Linda posts my DMs?

**DENISE**
Oh, everyone saw it. It was so funny. My husband says "sandwich plans" now when he doesn't want to do things.

**AARON**
Tell your husband he's using it wrong. There's no wrong way. There's no way. It's not a thing.

**DENISE** (delighted)
See? That's exactly the energy!

She's now filming him. On her phone. In the returns line.

**AARON**
Are you filming me.

**DENISE**
Just for the group chat! Say "sandwich plans"!

**AARON**
No.

**DENISE** (to her phone)
He said no! He's so dry!

Aaron looks at Pat. Pat looks at Aaron. For one moment, the two of them share something real: they both want this interaction to end.

**PAT** (quiet, just to Aaron)
Look. I can do fourteen. Store credit. Don't tell anyone.

**AARON**
Fourteen dollars.

**PAT**
It's the best I can do without a manager, and the manager is... (glances down the counter) ...currently being yelled at about a blender.

Aaron considers the toaster. Considers fourteen dollars of store credit at a store he never wants to enter again. Considers Denise's phone, still pointed at him.

**AARON**
You know what. Keep the toaster. Keep the fourteen dollars. I'm good.

He sets the toaster on the counter and walks away.

**PAT** (calling after)
Sir! You can't just leave merchandise!

**AARON** (not stopping)
It's your toaster now. We're done here.

---

## ACT THREE

**INT. BIG-BOX STORE — PARKING LOT — DAY**

Aaron sits in his blue car. Engine off. He's decompressing — staring at nothing, which is his favorite activity.

His phone buzzes. The group chat. He doesn't open it. It buzzes again. And again.

Finally, against his will, he looks.

> **LINDA:** Aaron!! Denise says you were SO funny at the store!!
> **DENISE:** He told the returns guy "that's a coupon with extra steps" 😂😂
> **MARCUS:** coupon with extra steps LMAO
> **LINDA:** Our Aaron!! 🏘️❤️

Aaron closes his eyes.

**AARON**
I went in for a refund and became content.

His phone rings. Unknown number. He answers because at this point, why not.

**AARON**
Hello?

**PAT (V.O.)** (quiet)
Hey. Uh. It's Pat. From returns.

**AARON**
How did you get this number.

**PAT (V.O.)**
Denise gave it to the group chat. Look — you left the toaster, and technically I have to log it as abandoned merchandise, and there's a form, and —

**AARON**
Pat. It's a toaster.

**PAT (V.O.)**
I know. But the form needs a signature. Could you... come back in?

A long, long pause. Aaron looks at the store. Looks at his phone. Looks at the sky, which has no opinion.

**AARON**
...Neat.

**PAT (V.O.)**
Is that a yes?

**AARON**
That's a "neat," Pat.

He hangs up. Sits in the silence of his car. Then, very quietly:

**AARON**
Coupon with extra steps.

He almost smiles. Almost.

**SMASH TO BLACK.**

**END CARD:** white on black, wavy — **STAY TRIPPEDD**.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Format:** 2D throughout. No 3D device this episode (the device is recurring, not mandatory).
- **Continuity:** "Sandwich plans" follows Aaron from EP01 — his deadpan keeps escaping into the wild. Denise/Linda/the chat are recurring.
- **Pat** is a potential recurring character: the only person more tired than Aaron.
- **Voice direction:** underplay everything. The "coupon with extra steps" line is flat, not a zinger. The comedy is that he means it literally.
- **The toaster:** never fixed, never refunded, ends as store property. Aaron's victory is leaving. That's the whole win.
- Nothing in this draft is locked. Owner reads first, then voice, then boards.
