# MYSTERY COAST: GHOSTED
## Show Bible (Seed — owner-directed 2026-10-12, clarified 2026-10-12)

**Network:** TRIPPEDD

**Concept:** Scooby-Doo + Space Ghost Coast to Coast + Aqua Teen Hunger Force.

**The twist (owner clarification):** They're LAZY. They barely ever want to solve the mysteries.
They just so happen to solve them sometimes — mostly by accident. The rest of the time
they're just bullshitting around, acting like the ATHF and Space Ghost Coast to Coast cast.

Not superheroes. Not earnest. Just a group of idiots who stumbled into a mystery show
format and can't be bothered to do the job right.

**Animation style:** Hanna-Barbera (Scooby-Doo Mystery Inc. production materials as template).
Study repo: mhvnsnt/cn-gigaleak-scooby-doo-mystery-inc

**Tone:** ATHF energy — they live together, weird stuff happens, they react badly.
Space Ghost Coast to Coast energy — incompetent hosting, guests who shouldn't be there,
everything going wrong. The mystery is incidental.

**Status:** Concept locked. Full bible TBD.
