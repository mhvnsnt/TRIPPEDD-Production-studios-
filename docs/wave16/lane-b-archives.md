# TRIPPEDD Resource Pull Program — Wave 16, Lane B: Verified-PD Archives (Federal Agencies, Open-Access Museums, National Libraries, Newspaper Morgues)

Lane: archives with genuinely public-domain or CC0/CC-BY material usable as production background plates and reference — federal agency media beyond the already-covered NASA/NOAA-central/USGS/NARA/LOC, museum open-access portals beyond Getty/Rijksmuseum/Wellcome/AIC/SMK/NGA, national-library newspaper morgues beyond Chronicling America, and Nordic/European aggregators. Every license status verified against the archive's own rights page or an authoritative statement (see **License** line per entry). Verification date: 2026-10-07.

The Wave 14 lesson stands: "public archive ≠ public domain." This wave was built by checking each archive's own rights/terms page — and it documents the traps honestly (V&A, British Museum, National Gallery London, Wisconsin Historical Society, commercial stock houses).

**De-duplication:** checked against `docs/RESOURCE_CATALOG.md`, `docs/catalog_wave13_laneB.md`, `docs/catalog_wave14_laneB.md`, and `docs/wave12/lane-b-bgplates.md` before adding. Skipped as already covered: Smithsonian Open Access, Met Open Access, Cleveland Museum of Art, LACMA, Cooper Hewitt, Paris Musées, NYPL PD, DVIDS, Openverse, NPS photos, Bureau of Reclamation, CDC PHIL, USGS 3DEP, Trove, HathiTrust, Biodiversity Heritage Library, National Gallery of Art (DC), Harvard Art Museums, British Library Flickr, Wikimedia Commons, LOC National Screening Room, AmbientCG, Poly Haven.

Use of appropriate byline/photo/image credits is requested." (verified 2026-10-07 via fs.usda.gov disclaimers page). ## QUARANTINE CANDIDATES

Per lane instructions, candidates are listed here — `docs/LICENSE_QUARANTINE.md` is NOT edited by this lane.

- **OpenStreetMap (ODbL 1.0)** — planet-scale geodata tempting for map/BG plates (building footprints, road networks for matte-painting base maps). Usable with care; needs a quarantine ruling before wiring into the terrain pipeline.

- **Wikimedia Commons BY-SA subset** — many Commons files behind the ✅ PD entries above (Nationalmuseum, Fotothek) are CC BY-SA rather than PD; the puller must gate on each file's license tag, never on the collection's headline license.
