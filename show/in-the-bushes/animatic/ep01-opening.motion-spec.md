# In the Bushes — EP01 Opening Motion Spec

This is the executable blocking target for the first animated proof. It is intentionally limited animation: movement is concentrated in timing, posing, object arcs, camera motion, and reactions rather than constant character motion.

## 00:00–00:02 — Panic
- Hold the nighttime environment.
- Teens cross frame in two or three reusable poses.
- Add quick head turns toward the off-screen threat.
- Camera makes a subtle push to increase pressure.

## 00:02–00:03.5 — The line
- Cut tighter on the teens.
- One teen snaps their head toward the bush.
- Dialogue lands: **“IN THE BUSHES!”**
- Hold for a fraction before the throw so the decision reads.

## 00:03.5–00:05 — Throw
- Six-pack follows a readable upward arc.
- One loose/open can spins slightly ahead of the pack.
- Bush remains still until impact; do not pre-react it.

## 00:05–00:07 — Transformation
- Impact gets one sharp foliage shake.
- Beer splash spreads across the foliage.
- Busch wake pose appears through a short energy pulse.
- Settle quickly into stillness; the joke needs room to breathe.

## 00:07–00:08.5 — Look-away
- Busch wakes and scans.
- Eyes/face turn away.
- Body follows only slightly.
- This is the signature awkward beat and must not be merged with the title transition.

## 00:08.5–00:09.5 — Reaction hold
- Near-still frame.
- Tiny sway or blink only.
- Sound can carry the discomfort.

## 00:09.5–00:11.5 — Title
- Hard cut to the title card.
- `IN THE BUSHES` dominates the frame.
- Busch may remain subtly behind the lettering.
- No beverage-company logo or trademark artwork.

## Production test
The opening passes when a viewer can understand the entire origin without narration: teens are hiding, beer must disappear, the beer enters the bush, Busch wakes, Busch looks away, then the title arrives.
