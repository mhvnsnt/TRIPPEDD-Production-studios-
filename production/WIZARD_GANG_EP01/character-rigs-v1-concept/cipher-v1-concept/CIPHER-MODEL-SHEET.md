## 8. CHARACTER NOTES

- **The feral grin is the design, not an expression.** It survives every viseme — even "rest" grins. Never calm it down, never render a "calm neutral" Cipher. His neutral IS manic.
- **Black mouth interior is intentional.** The Blackheart liquid look = near-black interior with glossy ink highlights. It is NOT dirt, NOT a rendering defect. Never "clean" it to pink/red. (Surprised-card correction round 1 lost it; fixed. Guard this.)
- **No stubble.** He is clean-shaven. The sad-card generation drifted stubble in; it was corrected out. Stubble on Cipher = defect.
- **Heavy brow is mandatory.** Brows are thick and furrowed in EVERY card. Thin stylized brows = drift.
- **Skin is #7A4839.** If a generation lightens it, reject. Every time. No whitewashing, ever.
- **Teeth are natural off-white.** Unlike Ashes' gold grill — Cipher's teeth are NOT gold. Gold is for his pendant only.
- **Energy:** feral, zoned-out, whisper-to-shriek. He twitches, he stares, he crawls. The rig cards hold the design; animation notes hold the energy (see guide).
