Narrator: Clothed and Confused.
Narrator: Day one. Gary Thornfield enters the wilderness with everything he needs to survive. All fourteen layers of it.
Narrator: With no tools but his own two hands, Gary builds a raft. He uses forty logs. He needs six. Gary does not believe in enough.
Narrator: The creek runs ankle deep. To Gary, it might as well be the Zambezi. One wrong step, and his seventh layer gets wet.
Narrator: Dinner. A wild pig. Unbothered. Moisturized. In its lane. Gary has chased it for four hours. The pig has been walking.
Narrator: Gary survives another day. The layers stay on. His dignity remains pending. Next week, the desert.
