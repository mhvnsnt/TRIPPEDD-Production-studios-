2. **pipeline.md** — Production steps, style-specific.
3. **character-templates.md** — Blank model sheet, rig spec, viseme pack.

## Quick Picker

- **New TRIPPEDD segment?** → off-the-air-style (anthology) or robot-chicken-style (sketch)
- **New God Molecule spinoff?** → xavier-style
- **New kids' comedy?** → dexter-style, chowder-style, or ed-edd-n-eddy-style
- **New action show?** → teen-titans-style
- **New mascot/brand character?** → ppg-style
- **Prestige project?** → gumball-style

## The Law

**STEAL-LIKE-AN-ARTIST:** Study the technique, extract the method, apply to YOUR original characters and shows. Never copy, redistribute, or sell their assets. This is how all art is made.
