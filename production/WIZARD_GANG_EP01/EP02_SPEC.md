## Art styles (owner 2026-10-07)
- **2–3 art styles total.** Base = the CARTOONY style (Shadow Wizard Money Gang meme look) — that's the show's home base.
- **Painterly/detailed style SOMETIMES** — used at flagged dramatic beats (per EP01 storyboard grammar).
- Same style rules carry from EP01: cartoonier group-shot style is the base; detailed/painterly refs appear only at dramatic beats.
- Closer likenesses — every character matches their HAND-PICKED character card art, robed AND unrobed.
- Less AI slop.
- Same animation style, same art style, same characters.
- Dialogue + character talking interactions (not narration over screensaver).
- Better sound effects, music — like a REAL show. Inspirations: Aqua Teen Hunger Force, King of the Hill, Beavis and Butt-Head, Archer, Metalocalypse.

## The owner's critique lens (pre-announced — build against it)
His EP2 critique will target:
1. **Likeness alterations of the same character** — even within the same scene. One character = one face, one body, every shot.
2. **Characters not matching their character card art** — the cards he hand-picked and named are the law. Robed and unrobed versions must both match.
3. General "looked nothing like what was asked for and shown and chosen."

## Standing QC rules that apply
- S17: no repeated scenes / frozen segments.
- S18: hood-off faces must match locked likeness.
- S19: no duplicate characters in one scene (EP2 onward — EP1 exempt).

## Gates
- Script: the drafted dialogue/screenplay is the EP2 script, pending owner's approval. Visual production proceeds against the draft; voice work waits for approval.
- Character cards: the locked reference library in character-refs/ is the single source of truth for every likeness.

## Likeness enforcement (production law)
- Every generated shot must be identity-checked against the character's card BEFORE it enters the episode.
- Same-scene consistency: all shots in one scene generated/checked as a batch against the same card.
- Any shot where a character drifts from their card gets regenerated or cut — never shipped.
- **GLBs are identity anchors too, not just card art and attires.** The 3D model for each character/attire must match the same locked likeness — the card art, the attire refs, AND the GLB all describe ONE person. A GLB that doesn't look like the card is a defect.
