# In the Bushes — EP01 Origin Opening v2

## Target
Approximately 29.5 seconds at 24 fps. The original 11.5-second title beat remains intact; this pass adds connective story beats and physical business so the opening feels like a scene rather than a title sting.

## Beat 1 — Establish the trouble (0:00–0:03)
Wide nighttime exterior. The location feels quiet enough that the teenagers' movement immediately changes the energy. Bush is ordinary and visually unimportant at first.

**Movement:** distant light flicker, subtle environmental motion, three teen silhouettes enter quickly.

## Beat 2 — Hide (0:03–0:06)
The teens duck into the area around the bush and repeatedly look back. One nearly drops the six-pack. Another catches it. The bush remains completely normal.

**Comedy:** the bush is treated like a hiding place before anyone realizes it is about to become a character.

## Beat 3 — The beer problem (0:06–0:08.5)
They discover the six-pack is still in their hands. One whispers, “We gotta get rid of these.” A can rattles. Everyone freezes. One teen slowly looks toward the bush.

**Movement:** handoff of pack, can wobble, heads turn one at a time.

## Beat 4 — The title phrase (0:08.5–0:10.5)
A teen suddenly commits to the solution: **“IN THE BUSHES!”** The others react for a fraction of a second.

This remains the first spoken occurrence of the show's title.

## Beat 5 — The throw goes wrong (0:10.5–0:13.5)
The pack and loose cans fly. One clips a branch. Another bounces. An open can spills directly over the foliage.

**Movement:** exaggerated arcs, spin, branch recoil, small impact shake.

## Beat 6 — Something is happening (0:13.5–0:17.5)
The teenagers begin running away. Behind them, the bush is still. Beer drips. A leaf twitches. Another twitch. Then the entire silhouette gives one unnatural shudder.

**Important:** don't reveal Busch too quickly. Let the audience notice the bush before the characters do.

## Beat 7 — Busch wakes (0:17.5–0:20.5)
Eyes become readable. Foliage shifts into Busch's wake pose. He looks around as though waking from a nap he did not remember taking.

## Beat 8 — Processing (0:20.5–0:23)
Busch looks toward the escaping teens, then down at the cans. He pauses. His expression says he has several questions and absolutely no context.

## Beat 9 — Look-away (0:23–0:25.5)
Busch deliberately looks away. The reaction should feel like: “I don't know what I just witnessed, and I'm not getting involved.”

This beat stays separate from the title card.

## Beat 10 — Awkward silence (0:25.5–0:27.5)
Tiny sway/blink. Night ambience. Hold long enough for the audience to feel the awkwardness.

## Beat 11 — Title (0:27.5–0:29.5)
Hard cut to **IN THE BUSHES**. Busch remains subtly visible behind/around the lettering.

### Animation budget
The scene should remain economical: reusable teen poses, Busch pose swaps, object translations/rotations, branch shake, camera pushes, holds, and a small number of transformation effects. The added runtime should create story, not busywork.

### Continuity lock
**Teen panic → hide → realize beer is a problem → “IN THE BUSHES!” → throw → beer soaks bush → delayed supernatural movement → Busch wakes → processes → looks away → silence → title.**
