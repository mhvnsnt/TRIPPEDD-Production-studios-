# Likeness Drill Log — ricky-based

Tier A (likeness target): `../tpb-trio-promo.png` — the character as presented
in the Goodville world.
Tier B (production asset): this kit's parts, hand-built in the key-art
South Park cutout language.

## Pass 1 findings (own-eyes review of preview.png)
- Cigarette prop too small — read as an orange/white nub, not a cigarette.
- Slouch under-read: torso lean too subtle, shoulders nearly even.
- Greasy hair, stripes, goatee all read well — kept.

## Pass 2 changes
- Cigarette redrawn larger (120px long, 30px thick on 150px canvas) and
  re-centered on the right hand anchor (398, 566).
- Torso lean increased (parallelogram widened), stripe range extended.
- Kept: stringy dark-brown locks past ears, navy/cream horizontal stripes,
  chin-patch goatee + thin mustache, half-lidded eyes, khaki pants.

## Verification
- preview.png: slouch reads via leaning torso + uneven shoulders; cigarette
  clearly visible in right hand; stripes/hair/goatee all anchor the read.
- Promo: reads instantly as the Ricky riff.
