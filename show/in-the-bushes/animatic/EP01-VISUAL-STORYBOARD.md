# In the Bushes — EP01 Visual Storyboard

This storyboard is generated from the current working-branch production assets and the active teen performance rig. It is a visual planning artifact: each panel represents a selected frame/beat, not a final rendered frame.

## Critical story correction — locked

**The beer cans do NOT become a character. The BUSH becomes the character.**

The beer is the catalyst. The cans remain ordinary, unanimated props throughout the origin. Beer spills onto/into the bush, the foliage reacts, the bush transforms, and **Busch emerges from the bush** as the newly conscious character. There is no animated beer-can face, body, personality, or speaking can in this sequence.

## Staging locks

- Police presence is established before the run.
- Teens hide inside the alley.
- The bush is at the alley exit.
- “IN THE BUSHES!” happens before the throw.
- Teens physically run out of the alley before throwing.
- The cans are always ordinary props; they never become characters.
- Beer is the catalyst for the bush transformation.
- The foliage/bush is what reacts, transforms and becomes Busch.
- Busch is the only newly animated character created by the beer in the origin.
- Throw, bush transformation, Busch wake, look-away, reaction hold and title are separate beats.
- The active character source is teen_performance_v2.py; the old stick-figure origin sheet is not the active character performance source.
- Low-fi means economical animation, not unfinished acting.

## Visual-board correction notes

Any visual storyboard panel that depicts a sentient beer can, beer-can face, anthropomorphic can, or can character is incorrect and must be replaced. S09 should visually emphasize **the bush changing**; S10 should reveal **Busch emerging from that bush**. S11 should show ordinary cans on the ground as props, not characters.
