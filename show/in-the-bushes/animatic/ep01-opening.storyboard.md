# In the Bushes — EP01 Opening Storyboard

**Status:** Production-ready blocking pass  
**FPS:** 24  
**Sequence:** EP01-origin-opening

## S01 — Night / Panic (00:00–00:02)

Wide nighttime exterior. A bush dominates one side of frame. Teenagers enter from the opposite side, moving quickly and looking back toward approaching trouble. Keep faces mostly silhouetted; the bush remains visually ordinary.

**Animation:** held background, 2–3 teen poses, quick head turns, subtle camera push.

## S02 — The Phrase (00:02–00:03.5)

Cut closer on the teenagers. One teen realizes the beer has to disappear.

**Dialogue:** “IN THE BUSHES!”

The line is the title's first spoken occurrence. Hold a fraction after the shout before the throw.

## S03 — The Throw (00:03.5–00:05)

Six-pack and loose/open cans are thrown toward the bush. Use exaggerated object arcs rather than full-body animation. One can spins ahead of the pack.

## S04 — Beer / Transformation (00:05–00:07)

Beer hits the foliage. Liquid spreads through the leaves. The bush gives a small involuntary twitch, then a larger supernatural reaction. Foliage parts/rearranges just enough to reveal that something inside is now conscious.

**FX:** splash, brief glow/energy accent, branch shake, settle.

## S05 — Busch Wakes / Looks Away (00:07–00:08.5)

Busch becomes readable as a character. First conscious beat. He looks around, then deliberately looks away as if embarrassed by the situation.

**Important:** This look-away is its own joke beat and must not be swallowed by the transformation.

## S06 — Reaction Hold (00:08.5–00:09.5)

Hold on Busch. Let the silence and awkwardness land. Minimal movement: tiny sway or blink.

## S07 — Title Card (00:09.5–00:11.5)

Hard graphic transition into the title.

# IN THE BUSHES

Keep the title readable immediately. Busch can remain subtly visible behind/around the lettering, but the title must dominate. No beer-brand logo is used.

## Editorial Rule

The sequence must preserve this exact comedic chain:

**spoken “IN THE BUSHES!” → beer enters bush → transformation → Busch looks away → reaction hold → title card.**
