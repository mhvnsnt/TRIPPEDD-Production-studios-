# EPISODE PREMISE BANK

**Purpose:** one-line mundane Curb-style premises fitted to Aaron. Premises ONLY — no scripts, no beats. Owner picks which get written.
**Status:** PROPOSALS. Nothing here is canon until the owner approves a premise for writing.

1. **The Return** — Aaron tries to return a broken toaster without a receipt and refuses to perform the customer-service apology script; the standoff becomes store legend.
2. **The Break Room** — someone keeps eating his clearly-labeled lunch; his passive-aggressive notes escalate until the whole office treats them as literature.
3. **The Wi-Fi** — his internet dies during a storm and he has to ask the neighbor for the password; the neighbor takes it as the start of a friendship.
4. **The Dentist** — after a year of dodging, he finally goes; his refusal to make small talk convinces the hygienist he's judging her life choices.
5. **The Jury Duty** — he gets summoned and discovers he loves it because nobody is allowed to talk to him; he tries to get picked again on purpose.
6. **The Plus-One** — a coworker invites him to a wedding; every attempt to decline gets misread as humility until he's giving the toast.
7. **The Subscription** — he calls to cancel a subscription and his deadpan breaks the retention rep, who starts confessing personal problems instead of offering discounts.
8. **The Dog** — he agrees to watch a neighbor's dog for one weekend; the dog prefers him to its owner and the owner starts competing for the dog's love.
9. **The Potluck** — he brings chips to a neighborhood potluck; his chips get praised as "restrained" and he's put in charge of next year's event.
10. **The Compliment** — a cashier compliments his shirt and he says "thanks" too quietly; she spends the transaction trying to figure out if he heard her.
11. **The Oil Change** — waiting at the mechanic, he won't make conversation; the mechanic decides he's "mysterious" and gives him a discount he never asked for, which bothers him more than paying full price would have.
12. **The Mailbox** — (from his actual podcast bit) Aaron's deadpan mail-checking routine gets filmed by a neighbor; it goes locally viral as "ambient content" and a brand wants to sponsor his mailbox.
13. **The Book** — someone at a party finds out he wrote books and spends the night treating him like a tortured genius; he spends the night trying to get back to the chip bowl.
14. **The Watch Party** — dragged to a wrestling watch party, he makes one quiet correct prediction and is treated as a wrestling oracle for the rest of the night.
