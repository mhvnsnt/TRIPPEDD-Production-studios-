## Core method: Off the Air

**Read first:** `~/workspace/animation-techniques/steal-like-artist/adult-swim/study-notes/off-the-air-method.md`

Dave Hughes' system IS the TRIPPEDD episode pipeline:
- **Bucket system** — collect clips per theme/segment until a bucket fills, that's a block
- **Music-first editing** — find the track, cut to it; transitions keep it moving
- **Sourcing** — Prelinger Archives + web deep cuts + owner's footage + show clips
- **VFX glue** — datamoshing, glitch effects until "you didn't quite know what you were looking at"
- **Licensing** — track down rights holders, treat work with respect
