## Production Pipeline (documented)

**Sources:**
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foster's_Home_for_Imaginary_Friends (production section)
- https://www.animationmagazine.net/2013/09/a-walk-on-the-bright-side/ (McCracken interview)

**The pipeline:**
1. Created by **Craig McCracken** (also created Powerpuff Girls)
2. Art direction: late 60s psychedelia meets Victorian styling — "trippy poster designs"
3. Software: **Adobe Illustrator + Flash + After Effects** — pure Adobe vector pipeline
4. Production split: **Cartoon Network Studios** (Burbank) + **Boulder Media** (Dublin, Ireland)
5. McCracken directed, executive produced, and story edited every episode
6. Music: "psychedelic ragtime" by James L. Venable

**Why this matters for Wizard Gang:** This is the closest pipeline match to our Flash-era workflow. Foster's proved you could do broadcast-quality character animation entirely in Illustrator/Flash/After Effects — the exact stack the owner already works in. The Boulder Media connection also overlaps with Gumball's pipeline.

## Key Takeaways for Our Pipeline

1. **Full Adobe vector pipeline** (Illustrator → Flash → After Effects) is proven for TV-quality character work
2. **Two-day story breaking** for 11 minutes — the McCracken story process is a model for our episode planning
3. **Character-first development** — McCracken's philosophy: compelling characters are the delivery system for comedy, not complicated mythology
4. **Artist-driven environment** — keeping talent employed across shows builds institutional knowledge
