### The Immutable Laws

Verified production laws only (these are owner rules already on record — they
govern HOW the show is made, not what its world is):

1. **Ashes keeps his diamond-grill grin/mouth in every style.** The grin is the
   signature; `X_REST` in the rig IS the grill grin.
   (Sources: `ORIGINAL-PIPELINE-V1-FROZEN.md` step 8; `WG-RIG-TEMPLATE.md` §4/§6.)
2. **Pipeline One stays as the base layer.** Chatterbox voices, Viggle AI body
   replacement, Muse video generation as a base layer, and the pan-and-zoom
   parallax shots all carry into the new real-cartoon pipeline. The V2 stack wires
   AROUND Pipeline One; it does not replace it.
   (Sources: `ORIGINAL-PIPELINE-V1-FROZEN.md` header/status; `WG-PRODUCTION-METHOD.md` intro.)
3. **Voices are Chatterbox clones laid in post; motion clips are delivered silent.**
   The generator must never invent a voice. The owner hears and approves every
   voice before it goes in an episode.
   (Sources: `docs/wizard-gang/pipeline-one/PIPELINE-ONE-MOTION-METHOD.md` §4;
   `WG-PRODUCTION-METHOD.md` step 1; `ORIGINAL-PIPELINE-V1-FROZEN.md` step 7.)
4. **A little drift is fine — the show is interdimensional television. Robed
   versions drift least. UNROBED versions must hold face / body / skin / eyes /
   character design.** The hood-off identity-change scene is a deliberate comedic
   gag, not drift.
   (Source: `ORIGINAL-PIPELINE-V1-FROZEN.md`, "Identity/drift rule", owner 2026-10-29.)
5. **AI makes PIECES. Animation runs on the cutout method.** Generative AI may
   ASSIST (in-betweens, cleanup) — never as sole animator. V1 is the exception
   that proved the rule; V2 is built on it.
   (Source: `ORIGINAL-PIPELINE-V1-FROZEN.md`, "AI/ANIMATION BOUNDARY", owner 2026-10-11.)

## 5. TONE & STYLE GUIDE

### Art style (verified production facts only)

- 2D cartoon. V2 rig standard is Tahoma2D 1.6.3 cutout puppets, one master puppet
  per wizard, animated on 2s and 3s (8–12 unique poses/sec); holds are the style.
  (Source: `WG-PRODUCTION-METHOD.md` steps 4–5; `WG-RIG-TEMPLATE.md`.)

  pan/zoom parallax — the camera move runs the plates at different rates so the 2D
  world reads 3D. Every episode gets parallax moves, not just locked shots.
  (Source: `WG-PRODUCTION-METHOD.md` step 6.)

## 6. EPISODE STRUCTURE (method reference — owner to confirm what applies)

No episode structure exists on file for Wizard Gang. The method below is borrowed
STRUCTURE from the Samurai Jack / Primal craft extraction
(`mhvnsnt/samurai-jack-repo`, `craft-for-our-shows/CRAFT-EP01.md` — written for
Mochiichao EP01, method only, none of its content). It is listed here as a menu
of mechanics the owner can accept, change, or reject — NOT as WG decisions:

- Environment gets real minutes first (a location portrait before the story starts).
- One sense is the episode's channel (sound, sight — declare it, route beats through it).
- Rhythm, not coverage (fast-fast-fast-slow; process montages are content).
- Creature / side-character life carries meaning (pose and eyes, not gesture).
- Cameos rationed (a figure at the edge of a held frame, gone when looked at directly).
- Color keys for light states (one graded master per location per light state).

Verified episode facts (production, not story):

- EP01 v10 runs 301 s with 51 inserts. (Source: `ORIGINAL-PIPELINE-V1-FROZEN.md`.)
- First proof shot spec `WG_V2_PROOF_001` is 72 frames (3.0 s) at 24 fps on 2s.
  (Source: `FIRST-SHOT-SPEC.md`.)

> **OWNER DECISION:**
>
> - Q: How long is an episode — EP01's ~5 minutes, or longer?
> - Q: Does every episode run the same machine (same open, same beats), or is each one shaped fresh?
> - Q: Which of the mechanics above, if any, fit Wizard Gang — and what is WG's own episode shape instead?
