## Mars (the character)

**Form**
- Appears as a **disembodied floating head** in black / space-like void with small sparkly stars.
- Production asset is a **complete head + neck geometry** (not a billboard face) so jaw, oral cavity, and camera moves work.
- **Identity lock:** `MARS_CANONICAL` — photographs / scanned likeness outrank generation. Style may change; face may not silently drift.

**Look**
- Skin: saturated **cobalt / electric blue** with cyan interaction, dimensional shading, granular digital breakup — not generic blue.
- Hair: **indigo / violet dreadlocks** (can disappear into black space).
- Eyes: **solid glowing white** — graphic, not realistic sclera/iris.
- **Forehead sigil** embossed into skin.
- Optional finish: low-res, low-FPS, limited palette, banded / early-3D / **Tonnō**-adjacent treatment.

**Personality**
- deadpan · overconfident · philosophical · stoner-adjacent · sincere · wrong · capable of terror and stupidity in the same line  
- Can be narrator, protagonist, observer, creator, prisoner, god, idiot, and audience **at once**.

**Performance rules**
- Comedy and philosophy coexist; then he makes a stupid joke.
- Never explain a mystery only because the system can.
- Lip-sync and mouth aperture are anatomy + real audio timing when claimed — not stickers.

## Cosmology

Mars is not “carrying a universe in his head” as metaphor only.  
**His consciousness is a cosmological substrate.**

Scale stack (Show Bible):

> thoughts → memories → simulated realities → dimensions → universes → multiverses → galaxies → microscopic worlds → quantum-scale phenomena → abstract mathematical spaces

Central questions (asked, not answered):
- What is consciousness?
- What happens when the observer becomes part of what is observed?
- Is Mars experiencing the universe, or is the universe experiencing Mars?
- Is Mars containing everything, or is something containing Mars?

**Production consequence:** deterministic **world seeds**, nested scenes, Gaussian/photogram detail, 2D memory/broadcast layers, and hybrid plates are not just VFX convenience — they enact the cosmology.

## Episode structure & visual law

- Every episode may enter a **completely different reality**: cheap video game, psychedelic children’s program, cosmic horror, live-action/AI footage, pure abstraction.
- Reality transitions are a **storytelling mechanism**.
- Death may produce **progression**, not reset.
- Surrealism needs **internal logic** even when the audience cannot see it.
- Imperfection may be intentional; **technical failure is never automatically “style.”**
- Provenance and source assets always preserved.

### Episode 01 (Show Studio mock)

- **Title:** *Episode 01: The Recursive Room*
- **Synopsis:** Mars finds himself in a room that seems to exist entirely within his own thoughts.
- **Scene 01 — The Awakening:** near-black space, tiny colored stars, cyan rim light; white eyes CU; cobalt + indigo dreads; deadpan line *“Yeah. That happens sometimes.”*
- **Scene 02 — Tonnō Pass:** reality degrades to ~640×480 quantized retro digital dream; Mars realizes the universe is a cheap old video game.

Cold-open drafts (e.g. municipal planning inside the mouth) must **serve** Recursive Room / nested-consciousness thesis, not replace it.
