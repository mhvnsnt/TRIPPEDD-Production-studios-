## 8. CHARACTER NOTES

- **FULLY MASKED — the face is NEVER visible.** Not in close-ups, not in "emotional"
  moments, not ever. Any render showing skin/eyes/mouth under the mask is REJECTED.
- **The shark-tooth mouth is the character.** Jagged white zigzag, sharp points. Do NOT
  soften, round, or "fix" it into normal teeth. Do NOT replace it with a human mouth.
- **Hair strands are load-bearing.** The long black dreadlock-like strands hanging from
  the mask sides are in the likeness bible. If a generation drops them, regenerate.
- **Blue trim + tassels are fixed.** #1779EF trim on eye holes and mask edges; hanging
  blue tassels. Count and placement may vary slightly by shot — the PRESENCE is locked.
- **Ear protrusions:** pointed, fin-like, on the mask sides (blue-trimmed). Present in all cards.
- **Silent character.** No voice found yet. Visemes built anyway for future dialogue —
  when he finally speaks, the M/B/P rule applies to the mask mouth exactly as to a real mouth.
- **Eye glints are ice-blue, not warm.** Ashes' glints are pale white-yellow. Hollow's are
  #AEE7FB ice-blue. Do not cross-contaminate the two characters' eye cards.
- **Base attire (locked):** black full-body suit, white serpent/dragon chest emblem, white
  accent panels under arms. **Dragon attire (locked, owner-supplied):** black long-sleeve
  rashguard with blue dragon print, black kung-fu pants with blue dragon embroidery, black boots.
- **Robe form:** ORANGE robe (#FD5C1D) with HOLLOW pendant — recruiting-pitch scenes.
- **Style:** thick black outlines, flat cel shading. NO photorealism, NO Disney 3D.
