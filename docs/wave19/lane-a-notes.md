## Honest negatives — Pocket 1 (university music-library digitizations)

- **Morgan Library & Museum — Music Manuscripts Online** (www.themorgan.org/music, ~900 manuscripts / 42,000 pages): EXCLUDED from catalog — Morgan's own press release states images may be printed/downloaded for **noncommercial use only**. NC-only = straight no-go per doctrine (same rule as Mucom88). [Wave 19 Lane A]
- **Gallica (BnF)**: already cataloged (line ~14628, ❓) — skipped as dup. Terms note for the record: Gallica grants free non-commercial use; commercial reuse of PD scans requires a license/fee agreement with the BnF. [Wave 19 Lane A]
- **Polona (Polish National Library)**: already cataloged (Wave 18 Lane A, ⚠️ per-item rights) — skipped as dup; pypolona (MIT) added instead as the tooling layer. [Wave 19 Lane A]
- **Music Treasures Consortium vs. existing Morgan entry**: the existing "Morgan Library — music manuscripts ❓" entry stays; the new MTC entry is the consortium portal layer, distinct scope. [Wave 19 Lane A]

## Honest negatives — Pocket 3 (military-band PD recordings)

- **UK Royal Marines Band Service / other UK service bands**: EXCLUDED — UK Crown copyright (sound recordings © Crown, 50 years from release; reuse needs licensing). US-federal-PD doctrine does NOT extend to UK bands. [Wave 19 Lane A]
- **Australian Defence Force bands**: EXCLUDED — Australian Crown copyright (50 years). Same reasoning. [Wave 19 Lane A]
- **West Point Band**: still unverified (no results this pass) — future-lane candidate; US federal PD likely but unconfirmed. **US Army Old Guard Fife and Drum Corps: VERIFIED ✅ this pass** (fifeanddrum.army.mil Terms of Use: site content is public information, freely distributable) — cataloged as an entry.
