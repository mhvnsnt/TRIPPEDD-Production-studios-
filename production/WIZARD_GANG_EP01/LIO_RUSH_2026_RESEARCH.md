## 1. Persona summary

In March 2026 Lio Rush adopted the **"Blackheart"** persona in AEW/ROH — a demonic alter ego he previously used on the independent scene (CZW/GCW, 2020–2021), now in "full force" and seemingly having "fully taken over" the normal Lio (TV Tropes). Debut: AEW Revolution 2026, 21-Man Blackjack Battle Royal (Zero Hour pre-show); first singles match as Blackheart: AEW Collision "Slam Dunk Sunday," March 22, 2026, vs. Tommaso Ciampa. He won the ROH World Television Championship from AR Fox at Global Wars (June 13, 2026, aired June 18), introducing a custom black-painted title, and lost it to Action Andretti at Death Before Dishonor (Aug 21, 2026). He also carried the persona into GCW (vs. Shotzi at GCW Amerikaz Most Wanted 2026).

**Look:** shaved head, white/black contact lenses, black liquid spilling from his mouth, body and face paint, new entrance theme, and an umbrella he carries while professing about rain.

**Behavioral notes (WhatCulture, March 2026):** "Crawling around on all fours, sporting white contact lenses, acting generally erratically, referring to himself as the Blackheart, and stating that 'he' is listening" — the Zero Hour commentary team compared him to **Gollum** from The Lord of the Rings. (Rush himself discussed watching LOTR for the first time on X in 2025 — wrestlinginc.) AEW's TikTok clips of the character repeatedly caption him "completely unhinged" (164K+ likes on the top clip).
